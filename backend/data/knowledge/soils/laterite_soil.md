# Soil Type: Laterite Soil (Oxisols & Ultisols)

## 1. Physical & Chemical Characteristics
- **Geological Origin:** Formed under tropical conditions with alternating wet and dry seasons; intense chemical leaching washes down silica and bases, leaving behind hydrated iron and aluminum oxides.
- **Structure & Hardening:** Soft and workable when moist, but irreversibly hardens into stone-like blocks upon prolonged sun exposure.
- **Texture:** Coarse-grained, vesicular, gravelly clay loam with low bulk density and high porosity.
- **pH Range:** 4.2 - 5.8 (Strongly acidic).

## 2. Inherent Nutrient Profile
- **Base Saturation:** Extremely low (< 25%); severely depleted in Calcium, Magnesium, and Potassium.
- **Phosphorus Fixation:** Very high phosphate fixation capacity; soluble orthophosphates convert to insoluble Aluminum Phosphate and Iron Phosphate.
- **Organic Matter:** Low to medium; organic residues decompose rapidly under high tropical heat.
- **Micronutrients:** Toxic concentrations of Aluminum and Manganese can occur at pH < 4.8; Boron and Molybdenum are severely deficient.

## 3. Soil Amendments & Health Management
- **Liming Protocol:** Apply agricultural dolomite or hydrated lime @ 2 - 4 tonnes/ha to raise pH above 5.5, precipitating toxic Aluminum.
- **Rock Phosphate Usage:** Apply slow-release Rock Phosphate (RP 20% P2O5) @ 200 - 300 kg/ha instead of pure soluble DAP/SSP, as natural acidity gradually solubilizes rock phosphate.
- **Silicate Slag & Organic Manure:** Apply basic slag @ 500 kg/ha and heavy FYM (15 tonnes/ha) to coat iron/aluminum oxide surfaces and reduce phosphate retention.

## 4. Primary Recommended Crops
- **Plantation Crops:** Tea, Coffee, Rubber, Arecanut, Cashew, Coconut, Cardamom, Black Pepper.
- **Fruits:** Pineapple, Banana, Citrus, Avocado.
- **Field Crops:** Upland Rice, Tapioca (Cassava), Sweet Potato.
