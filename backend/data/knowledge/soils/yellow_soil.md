# Soil Type: Yellow Soil (Hydrated Alfisols & Ultisols)

## 1. Physical & Chemical Characteristics
- **Geological Origin:** Develops alongside red soils in regions with higher moisture, higher humidity, or poor local drainage where ferric oxides hydrate into limonite.
- **Coloration:** Distinct yellowish-brown to pale yellow hue in surface and subsurface layers.
- **Texture:** Silty clay to clayey loam; finer texture and higher plasticity than typical red soils.
- **pH Range:** 5.4 - 6.8 (Moderately acidic to neutral).
- **Moisture Retention:** Moderate (35% - 48%); better retention than coarse red soils.

## 2. Inherent Nutrient Profile
- **Nitrogen (N):** Low to Moderate (160 - 240 kg/ha).
- **Phosphorus (P):** Low (9 - 16 kg/ha).
- **Potassium (K):** Moderate (180 - 260 kg/ha).
- **Organic Carbon:** Low to Moderate (0.45% - 0.65%).
- **Micronutrients:** Zinc and Boron respond well to external fertilization.

## 3. Soil Amendments & Health Management
- **Subsurface Aeration:** Periodic deep subsoiling (30-40 cm) breaks dense subsurface pans and improves root penetration.
- **Integrated Nutrient Management (INM):** Combine 75% chemical NPK with 25% organic manure (FYM @ 5 tonnes/ha) + Biofertilizers (Azospirillum and Phosphobacteria).
- **Boron Correction:** Soil application of Borax (10.5% Boron) @ 10 kg/ha prior to sowing in cruciferous and oilseed crops.

## 4. Primary Recommended Crops
- **Field Crops:** Paddy (Lowland Rice), Maize, Groundnut, Soybean.
- **Pulses:** Pigeonpea, Blackgram, Chickpea.
- **Commercial:** Jute, Sugarcane, Sesame, Mustard.
- **Vegetables:** Cauliflower, Cabbage, Chilli, Tomato, Ginger.
