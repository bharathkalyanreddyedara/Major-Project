# Soil Type: Alluvial Soil (Entisols & Inceptisols)

## 1. Physical & Chemical Characteristics
- **Geological Origin:** Formed by silt and sediment deposition by river systems (Indo-Gangetic plains, Godavari, Krishna, Cauvery deltas).
- **Soil Texture:** Varies from sandy loam to silty clay loam; highly porous, friable, and well-drained.
- **Water Holding Capacity:** Moderate to high (35% - 45%), permeable with excellent aeration.
- **pH Range:** 6.5 - 8.2 (Neutral to mildly alkaline).
- **Cation Exchange Capacity (CEC):** 15 - 30 cmol(+)/kg.

## 2. Inherent Nutrient Profile
- **Nitrogen (N):** Low to Moderate (200 - 280 kg/ha). Requires regular replenishment via split applications.
- **Phosphorus (P):** Moderate (15 - 25 kg/ha). Available form depends on pH.
- **Potassium (K):** High to Abundant (280 - 450 kg/ha) due to potash-bearing illite minerals.
- **Micronutrients:** Zinc (Zn) deficiency is widespread (especially in intensive rice-wheat rotations); adequate in Iron (Fe) and Copper (Cu).

## 3. Soil Amendments & Health Management
- **Zinc Management:** Apply Zinc Sulphate (ZnSO4 21%) @ 25 kg/ha as a basal dressing once every 2 years.
- **Organic Matter Maintenance:** Incorporate green manure crops like Dhaincha (Sesbania aculeata) or Sunnhemp at 45 days before puddling to restore soil organic carbon (SOC target > 0.75%).
- **Crop Rotation:** Implement cereal-legume rotations (e.g., Rice - Chickpea or Maize - Mungbean) to prevent nutrient exhaustion and break pest cycles.

## 4. Primary Recommended Crops
- **Cereals:** Rice, Wheat, Maize, Barley.
- **Cash Crops:** Sugarcane, Cotton, Jute, Tobacco.
- **Pulses & Oilseeds:** Chickpea, Lentil, Mustard, Groundnut, Sesame.
- **Horticulture:** Potato, Tomato, Banana, Mango, Guava.
