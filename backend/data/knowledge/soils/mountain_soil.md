# Soil Type: Mountain & Forest Soil (Inceptisols & Spodosols)

## 1. Physical & Chemical Characteristics
- **Geological Origin:** Formed in mountainous regions, hill slopes, and forested highlands (Himalayan belt, Western Ghats, Nilgiris).
- **Soil Profile:** Immature, shallow-to-medium depth profile over rocky parent strata; steep topography promotes gravitational movement and surface runoff.
- **Texture:** Silty loam to gravelly loam with high stone fraction; spongy upper horizon rich in raw humus.
- **pH Range:** 4.8 - 6.2 (Moderately acidic).
- **Moisture Retention:** High in undisturbed forest belts (40% - 55%), but susceptible to rapid erosion upon deforestation.

## 2. Inherent Nutrient Profile
- **Nitrogen & Organic Carbon:** High to Very High (SOC 1.2% - 3.5%); however, slow mineralization occurs due to low ambient soil temperatures.
- **Phosphorus (P):** Low to Medium (10 - 20 kg/ha).
- **Potassium (K):** Medium (180 - 280 kg/ha).
- **Micronutrients:** Rich in available Iron and Manganese; Zinc and Boron deficiencies appear in cultivated fruit orchards.

## 3. Soil Amendments & Health Management
- **Terrace & Contour Farming:** Establish bench terraces, contour bunds, and vegetative barrier strips (Vetiver grass) to curb topsoil runoff.
- **Bio-decomposer Inoculation:** Apply fungal decomposers (Trichoderma viride) and mycorrhizal inoculants (VAM) to accelerate leaf litter breakdown and phosphorus uptake.
- **Lime Dosing:** Apply agricultural lime @ 1.5 tonnes/ha once every 3 years in commercial temperate fruit orchards.

## 4. Primary Recommended Crops
- **Temperate Fruits:** Apple, Pear, Peach, Plum, Walnut, Apricot, Cherry.
- **Spices & Plantations:** Cardamom, Saffron, Ginger, Turmeric, Black Pepper.
- **Highland Crops:** Potato, Off-season Cabbage, Cauliflower, Barley, Buckwheat.
- **Beverage Crops:** High-altitude Specialty Tea (Darjeeling, Kangra, Nilgiris).
