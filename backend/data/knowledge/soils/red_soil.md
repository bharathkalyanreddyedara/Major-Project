# Soil Type: Red Soil (Alfisols & Inceptisols)

## 1. Physical & Chemical Characteristics
- **Geological Origin:** Formed by the in-situ weathering of ancient crystalline and metamorphic rocks (granites, gneisses) in warm, semi-arid regions.
- **Color Origin:** Characteristic red coloration is due to high concentrations of ferric iron oxides (Fe2O3, Hematite, Goethite).
- **Soil Texture:** Loamy sand to sandy clay loam; open structure, non-plastic, porous, and highly permeable.
- **Moisture Retention:** Low to moderate (25% - 35%); dries out quickly, necessitating frequent light irrigations or drip fertigation.
- **pH Range:** 5.5 - 7.0 (Slightly acidic to neutral).

## 2. Inherent Nutrient Profile
- **Nitrogen (N):** Low (120 - 200 kg/ha). Rapid leaching of nitrate ions occurs in sandy profiles.
- **Phosphorus (P):** Low (8 - 15 kg/ha). Subject to fixation by free iron and aluminum oxides in acidic pockets.
- **Potassium (K):** Low to Medium (150 - 250 kg/ha).
- **Organic Carbon:** Critically low (< 0.4%), leading to poor aggregate stability.
- **Micronutrients:** Adequate in Iron and Manganese; deficient in Boron (B), Zinc (Zn), and Sulphur (S).

## 3. Soil Amendments & Health Management
- **Liming / Acid Neutralization:** In soils with pH < 5.8, apply agricultural lime (CaCO3) or Dolomite @ 1 - 2 tonnes/ha based on soil buffer capacity tests.
- **Mulching & Moisture Conservation:** Use organic straw mulches or plastic mulch (25-30 micron) to retard surface evaporation and lower root-zone temperature.
- **Split Fertilizer Dosing:** Split nitrogen and potash into 3-4 split applications across the crop lifecycle to prevent deep percolation losses.

## 4. Primary Recommended Crops
- **Pulses & Legumes:** Groundnut, Pigeonpea, Cowpea, Green Gram.
- **Millets:** Finger Millet (Ragi), Foxtail Millet, Pearl Millet.
- **Horticultural Crops:** Mango, Guava, Pomegranate, Cashew, Tomato, Brinjal, Chilli.
- **Plantation:** Rubber, Coffee, Tea (in higher altitude red lateritic belts).
