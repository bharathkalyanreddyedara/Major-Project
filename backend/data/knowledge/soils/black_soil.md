# Soil Type: Black Soil (Vertisols / Regur Soil)

## 1. Physical & Chemical Characteristics
- **Geological Origin:** Weathering of volcanic basalt rock in the Deccan Plateau (Maharashtra, Madhya Pradesh, Gujarat, Telangana, Karnataka).
- **Clay Mineralogy:** Dominated by 2:1 expanding Montmorillonite clay (35% - 60% clay content).
- **Self-Mulching Property:** Highly plastic and sticky when wet; contracts drastically upon drying, developing deep vertical fissures (up to 1m deep) that enhance self-aeration.
- **Moisture Retention:** Exceptionally high water retention capacity (50% - 65%), enabling successful post-monsoon rainfed cropping (Rabi season).
- **pH Range:** 7.5 - 8.6 (Alkaline with free calcium carbonates).

## 2. Inherent Nutrient Profile
- **Nitrogen (N):** Low (150 - 220 kg/ha). High volatilization risk if urea is applied on wet surface without soil incorporation.
- **Phosphorus (P):** Low to Medium (10 - 18 kg/ha). Fixation by Calcium ions into insoluble Calcium Phosphate occurs readily.
- **Potassium (K):** High to Very High (300 - 550 kg/ha).
- **Secondary & Micronutrients:** Abundant in Calcium (Ca) and Magnesium (Mg); prone to Iron (Fe) chlorosis and Zinc (Zn) deficiency in high-pH calcareous pockets.

## 3. Soil Amendments & Health Management
- **Drainage Management:** Slow internal drainage makes vertisols vulnerable to surface waterlogging during heavy monsoon rains. Adopt Broad Bed and Furrow (BBF) systems or Ridge-and-Furrow planting.
- **Phosphorus Placement:** Always band-place phosphatic fertilizers (DAP/SSP) 3-5 cm below and beside the seed rather than surface broadcasting to minimize calcium fixation.
- **Organic Amendment:** Apply Farm Yard Manure (FYM) @ 10 tonnes/ha or Vermicompost @ 2.5 tonnes/ha to improve soil tilth and microbial phosphate solubilization.

## 4. Primary Recommended Crops
- **Fibre Crops:** Cotton (prime "Black Cotton Soil").
- **Oilseeds:** Soybean, Sunflower, Safflower, Groundnut.
- **Cereals & Millets:** Sorghum (Jowar), Pearl Millet (Bajra), Wheat, Maize.
- **Pulses:** Pigeonpea (Arhar), Chickpea (Gram), Blackgram (Urad).
- **Commercial:** Sugarcane, Tobacco, Chilli, Onion.
