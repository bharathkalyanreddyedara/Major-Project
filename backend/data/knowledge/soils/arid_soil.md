# Soil Type: Arid / Desert Soil (Aridisols & Entisols)

## 1. Physical & Chemical Characteristics
- **Geological Origin:** Formed in arid and semi-arid regions (Western Rajasthan, Southern Haryana, Kutch) where evapotranspiration drastically exceeds precipitation.
- **Soil Texture:** Coarse sand to loamy sand; single-grain structure with non-existent aggregation.
- **Moisture Retention:** Very low (10% - 20%); high infiltration rate (> 15 cm/hr).
- **pH Range:** 7.8 - 9.2 (Alkaline, with presence of Calcium Carbonate kankar pan at 30-100 cm depth).
- **Salinity / Sodicity:** Prone to secondary salinization with Electrical Conductivity (EC) often exceeding 4.0 dS/m.

## 2. Inherent Nutrient Profile
- **Nitrogen (N):** Critically low (< 100 kg/ha). Organic carbon is negligible (< 0.2%).
- **Phosphorus (P):** Low to Normal (8 - 18 kg/ha).
- **Potassium (K):** Moderate to High (200 - 350 kg/ha) due to potassium feldspars.
- **Micronutrients:** Zinc, Iron, and Copper are rendered unavailable due to high alkaline carbonate buffering.

## 3. Soil Amendments & Health Management
- **Gypsum Application:** For sodic arid soils with Exchangeable Sodium Percentage (ESP > 15%), apply agricultural Gypsum @ 3 - 5 tonnes/ha followed by fresh water leaching.
- **Micro-Irrigation:** Mandatory use of Drip Irrigation and subsurface fertigation to minimize evaporation losses by 50-60%.
- **Shelterbelts & Sand Dune Stabilization:** Plant windbreaks (Prosopis cineraria, Acacia tortilis) along field borders to reduce wind erosion.

## 4. Primary Recommended Crops
- **Drought-Hardy Millets:** Pearl Millet (Bajra), Sorghum.
- **Arid Pulses:** Mothbean, Cluster Bean (Guar), Cowpea.
- **Oilseeds:** Mustard, Sesame, Castor.
- **Arid Horticulture:** Date Palm, Ber (Jujube), Pomegranate, Aonla, Cumin, Coriander.
