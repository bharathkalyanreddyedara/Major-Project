# Agricultural Knowledge Base: Soil Health & Fertilizer Practices

## Soil Properties Interpretation:
- Soil pH:
  - < 5.5: Strongly Acidic. Apply Agricultural Lime (CaCO3) or Dolomite to increase pH.
  - 6.0 - 7.5: Neutral / Optimum for most cereal and cash crops.
  - > 8.0: Alkaline / Calcareous. Apply Gypsum (CaSO4.2H2O) and organic farmyard manure to lower pH.
- Nitrogen (N):
  - Low (< 140 kg/ha): Increase Urea application in split doses.
  - High (> 280 kg/ha): Reduce Nitrogen application to prevent lodging and succulence to pests.
- Phosphorus (P):
  - Low (< 10 kg/ha): Apply DAP or Single Super Phosphate (SSP) at root zone.
- Potassium (K):
  - Low (< 110 kg/ha): Apply Muriate of Potash (MOP) or Potassium Sulphate.

## Soil Classification & Suitability:
- Alluvial Soil: Highly fertile, rich in potash and humus. Best for Rice, Wheat, Sugarcane, Maize, Pulses.
- Black Soil (Regur): Rich in clay, calcium, carbonate, magnesium, potash. Ideal for Cotton, Soybean, Wheat, Millets.
- Red Soil: Porous, friable, deficient in nitrogen and humus. Excellent for Groundnut, Millets, Pulses, Tobacco with proper irrigation.
- Laterite Soil: Rich in iron and aluminium, acidic in nature. Suitable for Cashew, Tea, Coffee, Rubber, and Millets.
- Arid / Sandy Soil: Coarse texture, high salinity, low water retention. Suitable for Barley, Millets (Bajra), Dates with drip irrigation.
