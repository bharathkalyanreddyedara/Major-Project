# Integrated Pest & Disease Management (IPM): Rice (Paddy)

## 1. Major Insect Pests & Control Protocols
### A. Yellow Stem Borer (Scirpophaga incertulas)
- **Damage Symptoms:** "Dead hearts" in vegetative stage (central tillers dry up and pull out easily); "White ears" in reproductive stage (empty erect white panicles with chaffy grains).
- **Economic Threshold Level (ETL):** 2 egg masses/m2 or 10% dead hearts or 1 moth/m2.
- **Biological Control:** Release Trichogramma japonicum egg parasitoids @ 1,00,000/ha (5 cards) weekly starting from 30 days after transplanting (DAT). Install pheromone traps @ 20/ha.
- **Chemical Control:** Chlorantraniliprole 18.5% SC @ 60 ml/acre (150 ml/ha) in 200L water OR Cartap Hydrochloride 4G @ 10 kg/acre OR Fipronil 5% SC @ 400 ml/acre.

### B. Brown Planthopper - BPH (Nilaparvata lugens)
- **Damage Symptoms:** Nymphs and adults suck sap from stem base; circular patches of drying crop ("Hopper burn").
- **Cultural Control:** Provide "Alleyways" (30 cm paths every 2 metres of planting); avoid excess urea; drain water from field for 3-4 days to break reproduction.
- **Chemical Control:** Triflumezopyrim 10% SC @ 94 ml/acre OR Pymetrozine 50% WDG @ 120 g/acre OR Dinotefuran 20% SG @ 80 g/acre. Direct the spray nozzle towards the base of the plant canopy.

## 2. Major Diseases & Management
### A. Blast Disease (Pyricularia oryzae)
- **Symptoms:** Leaf blast (spindle-shaped lesions with brown margins and grey center); Neck blast (blackening and rotting of panicle neck, causing panicles to break).
- **Chemical Remedy:** Tricyclazole 75% WP @ 120 g/acre OR Isoprothiolane 40% EC @ 300 ml/acre OR Azoxystrobin 18.2% + Difenoconazole 11.4% SC @ 200 ml/acre.

### B. Bacterial Leaf Blight - BLB (Xanthomonas oryzae)
- **Symptoms:** Water-soaked lesions starting from leaf tips, progressing downwards with wavy margins; leaves turn straw-colored and roll inwards.
- **Control:** Copper Oxychloride 50% WP @ 400 g + Streptocycline (Streptomycin Sulphate) @ 15 g per acre in 200L water.
