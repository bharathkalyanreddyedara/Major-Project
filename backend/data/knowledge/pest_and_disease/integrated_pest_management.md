# Universal Integrated Pest Management (IPM) Principles

## 1. Core Pillars of IPM
1. **Cultural Controls:** Crop rotation with non-host families, deep summer ploughing to expose pupae and nematodes to solar heat, synchronous planting, clean bunds free of weed alternate hosts.
2. **Mechanical & Physical Controls:** Installation of light traps (@ 1/acre for night-flying moths), colored sticky traps (Yellow for Whitefly/Aphids, Blue for Thrips @ 25/acre), hand picking of gregarious caterpillar egg masses.
3. **Biological Controls:** Conservation of natural predators (Ladybird beetles, Chrysoperla carnea, Spiders); application of bio-pesticides (Bacillus thuringiensis - Bt @ 1.5 kg/ha, Beauveria bassiana @ 2 kg/ha, Trichoderma viride seed treatment @ 4g/kg seed).
4. **Targeted Chemical Control:** Applied strictly based on Economic Threshold Levels (ETL). Rotate chemical groups (IRAC MoA classification) to prevent pest resistance.
