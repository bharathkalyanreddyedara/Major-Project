# Integrated Pest & Disease Management (IPM): Cotton

## 1. Major Sucking Pests & Control Protocols
### A. Whitefly (Bemisia tabaci) & Aphids (Aphis gossypii)
- **Damage:** Sucks cell sap from leaf undersides, secretes sticky honeydew causing black sooty mold growth; vectors Cotton Leaf Curl Virus (CLCuD).
- **ETL:** 6-8 whiteflies per leaf or 10-15% infested plants.
- **Biological Control:** Install yellow sticky traps @ 25-30 traps/acre at crop canopy level. Spray Neem Oil (10,000 ppm) @ 2 ml/litre.
- **Chemical Control:** Diafenthiuron 50% WP @ 240 g/acre OR Pyriproxyfen 10% + Fenpropathrin 15% EC @ 250 ml/acre OR Dinotefuran 20% SG @ 60 g/acre.

### B. Thrips (Thrips tabaci) & Leafhoppers / Jassids (Amrasca biguttula)
- **Damage:** "Hopper burn" - leaves curl downward, margins turn yellow then reddish-brown; thrips cause silvering on lower leaf surfaces.
- **Chemical Control:** Flonicamid 50% WG @ 60 g/acre OR Fipronil 5% SC @ 400 ml/acre OR Acetamiprid 20% SP @ 40 g/acre.

## 2. Bollworm Complex Management
### A. American Bollworm (Helicoverpa armigera) & Pink Bollworm (Pectinophora gossypiella)
- **Pink Bollworm Detection:** Rosetted flowers with petals twisted like a rosette; premature boll opening; stained lint.
- **Management:** Install delta pheromone traps with Gossyplure septa @ 10-15 traps/acre. Release Trichogramma bactrae @ 60,000/acre.
- **Chemical Control:** Emamectin Benzoate 5% SG @ 100 g/acre OR Chlorantraniliprole 18.5% SC @ 60 ml/acre OR Spinetoram 11.7% SC @ 170 ml/acre.
