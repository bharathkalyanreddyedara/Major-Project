# Vegetable Disease Diagnostics & Organic/Chemical Remedies

## 1. Tomato & Solanaceous Crops
### A. Early Blight (Alternaria solani) & Late Blight (Phytophthora infestans)
- **Early Blight:** Concentric ring "target board" spots on older leaves. Remedy: Mancozeb 75% WP @ 2.5 g/L or Azoxystrobin 23% SC @ 1 ml/L.
- **Late Blight:** Water-soaked dark brown lesions with white cottony fungal growth on underside during cool, humid weather. Remedy: Metalaxyl 8% + Mancozeb 64% WP @ 2.5 g/L or Cymoxanil 8% + Mancozeb 64% WP @ 2 g/L.

### B. Tomato Leaf Curl Virus (ToLCV)
- **Symptoms:** Severe upward curling, puckering, vein clearing, stunting, and bushy appearance.
- **Vector Control:** Transmitted by Whitefly. Spray Cyantraniliprole 10.26% OD @ 1.8 ml/L or Thiamethoxam 25% WG @ 0.5 g/L.

## 2. Cucurbits & Gourds
### A. Downy Mildew & Powdery Mildew
- **Downy Mildew:** Angular yellow spots bounded by veins on upper surface, purplish downy growth on underside. Remedy: Fosetyl-Aluminium 80% WP @ 2 g/L.
- **Powdery Mildew:** White flour-like powdery coating on leaves and stems. Remedy: Wettable Sulphur 80% WDG @ 2.5 g/L or Hexaconazole 5% EC @ 1.5 ml/L.
