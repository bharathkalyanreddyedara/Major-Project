# Agricultural Water Pumps & Precision Drip Irrigation Design Guide
*Authoritative Reference: AgricultureGuruji & Precision Irrigation Directives*

## 1. Water Pump Selection Criteria by Farm Scale
- **Small Holdings (< 2 Acres):** 2 HP to 3 HP Monobloc / Submersible pump (discharge: 12,000 - 18,000 LPH).
- **Medium Holdings (2 - 5 Acres):** 5 HP to 7.5 HP Openwell / Borewell Submersible pump (discharge: 25,000 - 45,000 LPH, operating pressure: 2.5 - 3.5 bar).
- **Large Commercial Fields (> 5 Acres):** 10 HP to 15 HP multistage centrifugal pump paired with automated variable frequency drive (VFD) pressure regulation.

## 2. Filtration Network Architecture
- **Sand / Media Filter:** Indispensable when drawing water from open farm ponds, canals, or rivers containing algae and suspended organic matter.
- **Disc Filter (120 mesh / 130 micron):** Mandatory for borewell water containing fine silt and mineral particulates.
- **Hydrocyclone Filter:** Installed as primary filter when borewells pump significant coarse sand.

## 3. Drip Lateral Sizing & Emitter Sizing
- **Crops & Spacing Matrix:**
  - *Vegetables (Tomato, Chilli, Capsicum):* 16 mm inline drip lateral, 30-40 cm emitter spacing, 2.0 LPH discharge per emitter.
  - *Sugarcane & Banana:* 16 mm inline lateral, 40-50 cm spacing, 2.4 to 4.0 LPH discharge.
  - *Orchards (Mango, Citrus, Pomegranate):* Online adjustable button drippers (4.0 - 8.0 LPH) placed around the tree dripline basin.

## 4. Operational Diagnostics & Maintenance
- **Sub-main Pressure Gauge Monitoring:** Operating pressure at the head unit should be 2.0 - 2.5 kg/cm², and end-of-lateral flushing pressure must be >= 1.0 kg/cm².
- **Chemical Acid Treatment:** Acidification using commercial Hydrochloric acid (33%) or Phosphoric acid (85%) @ 1-2 Litres per 10,000 Litres irrigation volume every 60 days to dissolve lime scale encrustation.
