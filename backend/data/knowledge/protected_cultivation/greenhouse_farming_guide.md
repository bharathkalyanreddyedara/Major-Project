# Greenhouse & Protected Cultivation in India: Complete Agronomic Guide
*Authoritative Reference: AgricultureGuruji & ICAR Directives*

## 1. Structure Types & Cladding Specifications
- **Naturally Ventilated Polyhouse (NVPH):** Most economical and popular in India. Uses 200-micron UV-stabilized polythene film with anti-drip and anti-dust coatings. Side ventilation with 40-mesh insect-proof netting to exclude thrips and whiteflies.
- **Climate Controlled Greenhouse:** Equipped with cellulose evaporative cooling pads, exhaust fans, and micro-fogging systems (operates at 40-60 bar pressure with 10-15 micron droplet size) to drop ambient temperature by 6-10°C during peak summer.
- **Shade Net Houses:** 50% green or white-black shade nets used in hot tropical belts for nursery raising, ornamental foliage, and leafy greens.

## 2. High-Tech Climate Management (The Dutch Approach Adapted to Indian Belts)
- **Vapor Pressure Deficit (VPD):** Maintained between 0.8 to 1.2 kPa. High VPD (> 1.5 kPa) causes stomatal closure and blossom end rot, while low VPD (< 0.5 kPa) triggers fungal sporulation (Powdery Mildew & Botrytis).
- **Temperature & Humidity:** Daytime target: 24°C - 28°C; Nighttime target: 16°C - 19°C. Relative humidity kept at 65% - 75%.
- **CO2 Supplementation & Air Circulation:** Continuous horizontal air flow (HAF fans) prevents microclimatic dead zones and fungal hot spots.

## 3. Soil Preparation & Raised Bed Architecture
- **Raised Beds:** 90 cm top width, 30-40 cm height, and 50 cm walking path between beds.
- **Basal Soil Amendment per 1000 sq.m:**
  - Well-decomposed FYM / Vermicompost: 4 - 5 metric tons
  - Neem cake: 200 kg
  - Single Super Phosphate (SSP): 50 kg
  - Trichoderma viride & Pseudomonas fluorescens: 2 kg each mixed in FYM for root rot prevention.
- **Mulching:** 25-30 micron silver-black reflective film. Silver side facing upward reflects 30% sunlight, enhances lower canopy photosynthesis, and repels vector pests.

## 4. Drip Fertigation Regimen for Greenhouse Crops
- **Drip System:** In-line pressure-compensating (PC) drippers spaced at 30 cm interval with 2.0 LPH discharge rate per emitter.
- **Fertigation Timing:** Always fertigate during active transpiration (8:00 AM to 11:00 AM).
- **Flush Protocol:** Flush drip lines with 0.1% phosphoric acid / nitric acid once a month to dissolve calcium carbonate and iron precipitates.
