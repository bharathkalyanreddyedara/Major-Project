# 7-Day Hydroponic Green Fodder Production Guide
*Authoritative Reference: AgricultureGuruji Dairy & Fodder Protocols*

## 1. Principles of 7-Day Hydroponic Fodder
- **Concept:** Growing high-protein green fodder (Maize, Barley, Oats, Wheat) from seed to 20-25 cm grass mat in 7-8 days without soil using only water and light.
- **Conversion Efficiency:** 1 kg clean seed yields 7 to 8 kg of lush green, nutrient-rich biomass (crude protein: 14% - 18%, highly digestible ADF/NDF).
- **Water Savings:** Consumes 95% less water than traditional green fodder cultivation (2-3 Litres water per kg fodder produced vs. 80-100 Litres in open field).

## 2. Daily Production Cycle (Day 1 to Day 7)
- **Day 1 (Seed Selection & Priming):** Select certified disease-free maize/barley seed. Wash in 0.1% sodium hypochlorite solution for 5 minutes to sterilize fungus. Soak in fresh clean water for 12-14 hours.
- **Day 2 (Incubation / Sprouting):** Drain water, pack soaked seeds into aerated gunny bags in dark warm conditions (24°C - 26°C) for 24 hours to initiate white root radicle emergence.
- **Day 3 (Tray Loading & Micro-Sprinkling):** Spread sprouted seeds evenly onto food-grade polypropylene trays (1.0 kg seed per standard 60x30 cm tray). Set micro-foggers to mist for 30 seconds every 2 hours.
- **Day 4 - 6 (Rapid Vegetative Shoot Growth):** Provide 10-12 hours of ambient diffused light (or 40W LED agri-grow tubes). Maintain room temperature at 22°C - 25°C and relative humidity at 70% - 80%.
- **Day 7 (Harvesting the Fodder Mat):** Harvest dense 20-25 cm grass mat with intertwined clean root network. Feed complete mat (greens + roots + seed residue) directly to dairy cows, buffaloes, goats, and poultry.

## 3. Nutritional Impact on Dairy Cattle
- Increases milk yield by 10% - 15% and elevates milk fat (SNF) due to high enzyme activity and bioavailable vitamins (A, B-complex, E).
- Significantly lowers dairy feeding costs by replacing expensive commercial concentrates.
