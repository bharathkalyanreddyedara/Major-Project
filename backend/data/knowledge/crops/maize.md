# Crop: Maize / Corn (Zea mays)

## 1. Agro-Climatic Requirements & Soil Suitability
- **Optimal Soil Types:** Well-drained Loamy sand to Clay Loam, Alluvial, Red and Black soils.
- **pH Range:** 6.0 - 7.5. Sensitive to water stagnation.
- **Temperature Thresholds:** 21°C - 30°C.
- **Water Requirement:** 500 - 650 mm.

## 2. Growth Stages & Lifecycle Calendar (Sowing to Harvest: 95-110 Days)
### Stage 1: Sowing & Seedling (Day 1 - 20)
- **Key Actions:** Spacing 60x20 cm. Basal dose: 25% N + full P + full K + Zinc Sulphate @ 25 kg/ha. Spray Atrazine 50% WP @ 1 kg/ha as pre-emergence weed control.
### Stage 2: Knee-High Stage (Day 21 - 40)
- **Key Actions:** 1st Top Dressing: 50% Nitrogen (Urea). Install pheromone traps for Fall Armyworm (FAW).
### Stage 3: Tasseling & Silking (Day 41 - 65)
- **Key Actions:** 2nd Top Dressing: Remaining 25% Nitrogen. **Critical water stage**: Do not allow moisture stress during silking.
### Stage 4: Grain Filling & Milking (Day 66 - 85)
- **Key Actions:** Irrigate at grain filling. Spray Emamectin Benzoate 5% SG @ 80 g/acre if FAW larvae are observed in cob whorls.
### Stage 5: Physiological Maturity & Harvest (Day 86 - 110)
- **Key Actions:** Black layer forms at grain tip; sheath turns papery white. Harvest and shell cobs when moisture drops below 15%.
