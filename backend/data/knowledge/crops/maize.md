# Comprehensive Agronomic Guide: Maize / Corn (Zea mays)
*Authoritative Source: ICAR-IIMR (Indian Institute of Maize Research)*

## 1. Agro-Climatic & Soil Requirements
- **Scientific Name:** *Zea mays* L. (Family: Poaceae)
- **Optimal Soil Type:** Well-drained fertile sandy loam to clay loam, rich in organic matter. Sensitive to water stagnation.
- **Soil pH:** 6.0 - 7.5.
- **Temperature Thresholds:** 21°C - 30°C for optimal vegetative and cob growth.
- **Water Requirement:** 500 - 650 mm.

## 2. Varieties & Sowing Parameters
- **Popular Hybrids:** HQPM 1, HQPM 5 (Quality Protein Maize), Bio 9681, Pioneer 3396, NK 6240, DKC 9108, COHM 6.
- **Seed Rate:** Grain Maize: 8-10 kg/acre (20 kg/ha); Sweet Corn: 3-4 kg/acre; Baby Corn: 10-12 kg/acre.
- **Spacing:** 60 cm (row to row) x 20 cm (plant to plant).
- **Seed Treatment:** Cyantraniliprole 19.8% + Thiamethoxam 19.8% FS @ 6 ml/kg seed (for Fall Armyworm protection) + *Trichoderma* @ 4g/kg.

## 3. Crop Growth Stages & Critical Practices (95 - 110 Days)
### Stage 1: Germination & Early Vegetative (Day 1 - 20)
- **Field Actions:** Sowing at 4 cm depth. Basal dose: 25% N + full P + full K + Zinc Sulphate @ 25 kg/ha.
- **Weed Control:** Atrazine 50% WP @ 1.0 kg/ha applied as pre-emergence within 48 hours of sowing.
### Stage 2: Knee-High Stage - V6 (Day 21 - 40)
- **Field Actions:** 1st Top-Dressing: 50% Nitrogen (Urea @ 45 kg/acre). Earthing up to prevent lodging.
- **Pest Watch:** Scout whorls for Fall Armyworm (FAW) pinhole damage.
### Stage 3: Tasseling & Silking Stage - VT/R1 (Day 41 - 65)
- **Critical Action:** **Peak moisture sensitive window**: Moisture stress during silking causes poor pollination and incomplete grain setting. 2nd Top-Dressing: Remaining 25% Nitrogen.
### Stage 4: Milking & Grain Filling (Day 66 - 85)
- **Field Actions:** Maintain adequate soil moisture. Apply light irrigation.
### Stage 5: Physiological Maturity & Harvest (Day 86 - 110)
- **Maturity Index:** Black layer formation at base of grain; husk leaves dry and turn papery white. Harvest and dry cobs to 14% grain moisture.

## 4. Scientific Fertilizer Schedule (RDF: 120:60:50 N:P2O5:K2O kg/ha)
- **Basal:** 30 kg N (65 kg Urea) + 60 kg P2O5 (130 kg DAP) + 50 kg K2O (83 kg MOP) + 25 kg ZnSO4/ha.
- **1st Split (Knee High - 30 DAS):** 60 kg N (130 kg Urea/ha).
- **2nd Split (Tasseling - 50 DAS):** 30 kg N (65 kg Urea/ha).

## 5. Major Pests & Diseases
- **Fall Armyworm (*Spodoptera frugiperda*):**
  - *Damage:* Larvae feed inside whorls causing ragged leaves and large fecal pellets.
  - *Control:* Whorl application of Emamectin Benzoate 5% SG @ 0.4 g/L or Chlorantraniliprole 18.5% SC @ 0.4 ml/L or Spinetoram 11.7% SC @ 0.5 ml/L.
- **Maydis Leaf Blight (*Bipolaris maydis*):**
  - *Control:* Spray Mancozeb 75% WP @ 2.5 g/L or Azoxystrobin 23% SC @ 1 ml/L.
