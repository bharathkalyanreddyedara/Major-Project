# Comprehensive Agronomic Guide: Coffee (Coffea arabica & Coffea canephora)
*Authoritative Source: Central Coffee Research Institute (CCRI - Coffee Board of India)*

## 1. Agro-Climatic & Soil Requirements
- **Species:** Arabica (High elevation: 1000-1500m MSL, 15°C-25°C); Robusta (Lower elevation: 500-1000m MSL, 20°C-30°C).
- **Optimal Soil:** Deep, well-drained volcanic red laterite and forest soils rich in organic matter (pH 5.5 - 6.5).
- **Rainfall:** 1500 - 2500 mm. Blossom showers (March-April) and backing showers (April-May) are critical.

## 2. Shade & Plant Architecture
- **Two-Tier Shade Canopy:** Upper canopy (*Ficus*, *Albizia lebbeck*); Lower canopy (*Dadap* / *Erythrina lithosperma*).
- **Spacing:** Arabica: 2x2m (2500 plants/ha); Robusta: 3x3m (1100 plants/ha).

## 3. Fertilizer Schedule (kg/ha/year)
- **Arabica:** 120 N : 90 P2O5 : 120 K2O applied in 3 splits (Pre-monsoon, Post-monsoon, Post-harvest).
- **Robusta:** 100 N : 80 P2O5 : 100 K2O applied in 2 splits.

## 4. Pest & Disease Management
- **Coffee White Stem Borer (*Xylotrechus quadripes*):** Mechanical tracing and bark scrubbing in October and April.
- **Coffee Leaf Rust (*Hemileia vastatrix*):** Spray 0.5% Bordeaux mixture before monsoon (May-June) and post-monsoon (September-October).
