# Crop: Coffee (Coffea arabica & Coffea canephora / Robusta)
- **Soil Suitability:** Deep, porous, well-drained volcanic Red/Laterite and Mountain forest soils rich in humus (pH 5.5 - 6.5).
- **Shade Management:** Two-tier shade canopy (Permanent trees: *Ficus*, *Albizia*; Temporary: *Dadap*).
- **Fertilizer:** N: 120 kg, P2O5: 90 kg, K2O: 120 kg/ha applied in 3 splits (Pre-monsoon, Post-monsoon, Winter).
- **Pest & Disease:** White stem borer managed by mechanical tracing and bark scrubbing; Coffee Leaf Rust (*Hemileia vastatrix*) managed with 0.5% Bordeaux mixture.
