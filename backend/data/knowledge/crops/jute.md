# Comprehensive Agronomic Guide: Jute (Corchorus olitorius & C. capsularis)
*Authoritative Source: ICAR-CRIJAF (Central Research Institute for Jute and Allied Fibres)*

## 1. Soil & Climate
- **Soil:** Deep, rich alluvial delta soils with annual river silt replenishment (pH 6.0 - 7.5).
- **Climate:** Warm and humid (25°C - 35°C, relative humidity > 75%, rainfall > 1200 mm).

## 2. Sowing & Fertilizer Protocols
- **Cultivars:** JRO 204 (Suren), JRO 524 (Navin), JRC 517, JRO 8432.
- **Seed Rate:** *Olitorius* (Tossa Jute): 2.5 kg/acre (6 kg/ha); *Capsularis* (White Jute): 3.5 kg/acre. Spacing: 25x5 cm.
- **Fertilizer (RDF):** 60 kg N + 30 kg P2O5 + 30 kg K2O/ha.

## 3. Retting & Extraction
- **Harvesting:** Harvest at 120-135 days (small pod formation stage) for peak tensile fibre strength.
- **Microbial Retting:** Submerge reed bundles in slow-moving water with *CRIJAF Sona* microbial consortium for 12-15 days. Extract, wash, and sun-dry golden bast fibres.
