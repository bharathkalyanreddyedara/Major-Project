# Crop: Jute (Corchorus olitorius & Corchorus capsularis)
- **Soil Suitability:** Sandy loam and clay loam Alluvial delta soils with periodic river silt deposition (pH 6.0 - 7.5).
- **Fertilizer Recommendation:** N: 60 kg/ha, P2O5: 30 kg/ha, K2O: 30 kg/ha.
- **Retting & Harvesting:** Harvest at 120-135 days (small pod stage). Ret stalks in slow-moving fresh water for 15-20 days until bast fibres separate cleanly.
