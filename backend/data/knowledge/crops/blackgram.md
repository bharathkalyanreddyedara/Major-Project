# Comprehensive Agronomic Guide: Blackgram / Urad (Vigna mungo)
*Authoritative Source: ICAR-IIPR*

## 1. Soil & Climate
- **Soil:** Black cotton soils (Vertisols) and fertile alluvial loams (pH 6.5 - 7.8).
- **Seasons:** Kharif, Summer, and Rice-Fallow Rabi crop (utilizing residual moisture).

## 2. Varieties & Sowing Protocols
- **Cultivars:** PU 31, VBN 6, VBN 8, LBG 752, Shekhar 2, IPU 2-43.
- **Seed Rate:** Kharif: 6-8 kg/acre; Rice Fallow: 10-12 kg/acre broadcasted in standing paddy 3 days before harvest.
- **Fertilizer:** 20 kg N + 40 kg P2O5 + 20 kg K2O + 20 kg Sulphur/ha.
