# Crop: Chickpea / Bengal Gram (Cicer arietinum)

## 1. Agro-Climatic Requirements & Soil Suitability
- **Optimal Soil Types:** Well-drained sandy loam to clay loam (Alluvial, Black, Red soils).
- **pH Range:** 6.0 - 7.8. Highly sensitive to waterlogging and soil salinity.
- **Season:** Rabi (Cool and dry climate; 15°C - 25°C).
- **Water Requirement:** 250 - 350 mm (1-2 light irrigations).

## 2. Growth Stages & Lifecycle Calendar (90-110 Days)
### Stage 1: Sowing & Branching (Day 1 - 30)
- **Actions:** Inoculate seeds with Rhizobium + PSB cultures (200g/10kg seed). Basal application: 20 kg N + 40 kg P2O5 + 20 kg S/ha.
### Stage 2: Nipping / Topping & Pre-flowering (Day 31 - 50)
- **Actions:** Nip apical shoot tips at 35-40 days to promote profuse lateral branching. 1st light irrigation at pre-flowering.
### Stage 3: Pod Formation & Grain Filling (Day 51 - 80)
- **Actions:** 2nd light irrigation at pod initiation. Install T-shaped bird perches @ 20/acre and spray NPV @ 250 LE/ha or Emamectin Benzoate for Helicoverpa pod borer.
### Stage 4: Maturity & Harvest (Day 81 - 105)
- **Actions:** Harvest when leaves turn golden brown and pods rattle upon shaking. Sun-dry to 10% moisture.
