# Comprehensive Agronomic Guide: Chickpea / Bengal Gram (Cicer arietinum)
*Authoritative Source: ICAR-IIPR (Indian Institute of Pulses Research)*

## 1. Agro-Climatic & Soil Requirements
- **Scientific Name:** *Cicer arietinum* L. (Family: Fabaceae)
- **Optimal Soil Type:** Well-drained sandy loam, clay loam, alluvial and light black soils. Extremely sensitive to waterlogging.
- **Soil pH:** 6.0 - 7.8.
- **Season & Temperature:** Rabi (Cool dry climate; 15°C - 25°C).
- **Water Requirement:** 250 - 350 mm (1-2 critical irrigations).

## 2. Varieties & Sowing Protocols
- **Popular Varieties:** Desi: JG 11, JAKI 9218, Digvijay, Pusa 362; Kabuli: KAK 2, JGK 1, Pusa 1053.
- **Seed Rate:** Desi: 30 kg/acre (75 kg/ha); Kabuli: 40-45 kg/acre (100 kg/ha). Spacing: 30x10 cm.
- **Seed Inoculation:** Dual seed treatment with *Mesorhizobium ciceri* + PSB @ 200g each per 10kg seed + *Trichoderma viride* @ 4g/kg seed.

## 3. Growth Stages & Practices (90 - 110 Days)
### Stage 1: Sowing & Vegetative Branching (Day 1 - 35)
- **Actions:** Basal fertilizer: 20 kg N + 40 kg P2O5 + 20 kg S/ha. Pendimethalin 30% EC @ 1.0 L/acre as pre-emergence weed control.
### Stage 2: Nipping / Apical Topping (Day 35 - 45)
- **Critical Action:** Pluck apical terminal buds at 35-40 days to break apical dominance and stimulate 40% more pod-bearing branches.
### Stage 3: Flowering & Pod Initiation (Day 46 - 75)
- **Actions:** 1st critical irrigation at pre-flowering (Day 45). **Do not irrigate during peak flowering** (causes flower drop). Install T-perches (@ 20/acre) for predatory birds.
### Stage 4: Pod Filling & Maturity (Day 76 - 105)
- **Actions:** 2nd light irrigation at pod filling (Day 75). Harvest when leaves turn yellow-brown and pods rattle upon shaking.

## 4. Fertilizer Schedule (RDF: 20:40:20 N:P2O5:K2O + 20 S kg/ha)
- Full dose applied as basal: 44 kg Urea + 250 kg Single Super Phosphate (SSP provides both P and Sulphur) + 33 kg MOP/ha.

## 5. Major Pests & Diseases
- **Gram Pod Borer (*Helicoverpa armigera*):**
  - *Damage:* Larvae bore circular holes in pods and feed on developing seeds with half body outside.
  - *Control:* Spray Emamectin Benzoate 5% SG @ 80 g/acre or Chlorantraniliprole 18.5% SC @ 50 ml/acre or Indoxacarb 14.5% SC @ 200 ml/acre.
- **Fusarium Wilt (*Fusarium oxysporum* f. sp. *ciceri*):**
  - *Symptoms:* Drooping and yellowing of foliage, vascular browning in root split.
  - *Remedy:* Seed treatment with *Trichoderma harzianum* @ 5g/kg seed + deep summer ploughing.
