# Crop: Mungbean / Green Gram (Vigna radiata)
- **Soil Suitability:** Well-drained fertile Loams and Alluvial soils (pH 6.2 - 7.5).
- **Fertilizer:** N: 20 kg, P2O5: 40 kg, K2O: 20 kg/ha.
- **Disease Management:** Yellow Mosaic Virus (MYMV) transmitted by Whitefly; use resistant varieties (e.g., IPM 02-3) and control whitefly vectors.
