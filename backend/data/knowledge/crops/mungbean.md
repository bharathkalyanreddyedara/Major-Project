# Comprehensive Agronomic Guide: Mungbean / Green Gram (Vigna radiata)
*Authoritative Source: ICAR-IIPR*

## 1. Soil & Seasons
- **Soil:** Well-drained fertile loam to alluvial loam (pH 6.2 - 7.5).
- **Seasons:** Kharif, Rabi (South India), and Spring/Summer catch crop (after Potato/Sugarcane/Wheat).

## 2. Varieties & Sowing Protocols
- **Cultivars:** IPM 02-3, IPM 205-7 (Virat), Pusa Vishal, Samrat, SML 668, GAM 5.
- **Seed Rate:** Kharif: 6-7 kg/acre; Summer: 8-10 kg/acre. Spacing: 30x10 cm.
- **Fertilizer (RDF):** 20 kg N + 40 kg P2O5 + 20 kg K2O/ha.

## 3. Disease Management
- **Mungbean Yellow Mosaic Virus (MYMV):** Transmitted by Whitefly. Spray Thiamethoxam 25% WG @ 40 g/acre or Dimethoate 30% EC @ 300 ml/acre.
