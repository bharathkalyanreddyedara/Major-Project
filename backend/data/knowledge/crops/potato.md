# Comprehensive Agronomic Guide: Potato (Solanum tuberosum)
*Authoritative Source: ICAR-CPRI (Central Potato Research Institute)*

## 1. Agro-Climatic & Soil Requirements
- **Optimal Soil Type:** Well-drained, friable, loose sandy loam to alluvial loam rich in organic matter (loose soil allows unhindered tuber expansion).
- **Soil pH:** 5.2 - 6.5 (Slightly acidic soil prevents Common Scab disease).
- **Temperature:** Vegetative growth: 20°C - 25°C; Tuberization (critical): 16°C - 20°C. If night temperatures exceed 22°C, tuber initiation drops sharply.

## 2. Seed Tubers & Planting
- **Certified Varieties:** Kufri Jyoti, Kufri Pukhraj, Kufri Bahar, Kufri Chipsona-1/3 (processing), Kufri Himalini.
- **Seed Rate:** 1.5 - 2.0 tonnes/ha (well-sprouted, disease-free seed tubers of 35-45g size).
- **Seed Treatment:** Treat seed tubers with Mancozeb 75% WP @ 2.5 g/L or *Trichoderma viride* @ 10 g/kg to prevent seed-borne Black Scurf and Dry Rot.
- **Spacing:** 60 cm between ridges and 20 cm between tubers on ridges.

## 3. Growth Stages & Management (90 - 110 Days)
- **Emergence & Rosette (1-20 DAS):** Provide light pre-emergence irrigation; break crust if formed.
- **Stolon Initiation & Earthing-Up (25-35 DAS):** First earthing-up when plants reach 15-20 cm height. Top-dress 50% Nitrogen (Urea).
- **Tuberization & Bulking (40-75 DAS):** Critical water demand stage. Maintain 65-75% field capacity moisture. Second light earthing-up to prevent greening of exposed tubers (solanine synthesis).
- **Dehaulming (80-90 DAS):** Cut stems at ground level 10-12 days before harvest to harden tuber skin and prevent Late Blight spore wash onto tubers.

## 4. Fertilizer & Nutrient Management
- **NPK Dose:** 180 kg N : 100 kg P2O5 : 150 kg K2O per hectare + 25 tonnes FYM.
- **Basal:** 50% N + 100% P + 100% K (preferably SOP - Sulphate of Potash for better tuber dry matter and chip quality).
- **Top-dressing:** 50% N at first earthing-up (30 DAS). Spray 0.2% Borax + Zinc Sulphate (0.2%) at tuber initiation.

## 5. Pest & Disease Management
- **Late Blight (*Phytophthora infestans*):** Spray Mancozeb 75 WP @ 2.5 g/L preventatively. Upon symptom onset, spray Cymoxanil 8% + Mancozeb 64% WP @ 2.5 g/L or Dimethomorph 50% WP @ 1 g/L.
- **Potato Aphid (*Myzus persicae*):** Monitor aphid sticky traps (critical for seed crop). Spray Thiamethoxam 25 WG @ 0.4 g/L or Imidacloprid 17.8 SL @ 0.5 ml/L.
- **Potato Tuber Moth (PTM):** Keep tubers covered with soil (proper earthing-up). Install PTM pheromone traps in storehouses.
