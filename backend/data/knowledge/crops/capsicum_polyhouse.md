# Polyhouse Colored Capsicum (Bell Pepper) Cultivation & Fertigation
*Authoritative Reference: AgricultureGuruji Protected Cultivation Directives*

## 1. Varieties & Polyhouse Setup
- **Color Varieties:** Red (Bachata, Inspiration, Triple 5), Yellow (Orobelle, Bachata Yellow), Green (Indra).
- **Planting Density:** Raised beds (90 cm width, 40 cm height). Zig-zag 2 rows per bed with 45 cm row-to-row and 30 cm plant-to-plant spacing (approx. 3,200 to 3,500 plants per 1000 sq.m).
- **Trellising Architecture:** 2-leader or 4-leader trellising system using high-tensile GI wire and UV-stabilized polypropylene twine clipped with plastic vine rings.

## 2. Water-Soluble Fertigation Program (per 1000 sq.m greenhouse)
- **Vegetative Phase (Days 1 to 30 after transplanting):**
  - WSF 19:19:19: 1.5 kg / 1000 m² every alternate day.
  - Calcium Nitrate: 1.0 kg / 1000 m² once a week (prevents Blossom End Rot).
  - Humic acid (98%): 250 g / 1000 m² every 10 days.
- **Flowering & Fruit Development (Days 31 to 90):**
  - WSF 12:61:0 (MAP): 1.5 kg / 1000 m² twice a week.
  - WSF 0:52:34 (MKP): 2.0 kg / 1000 m² twice a week.
  - Calcium Nitrate + Boron (Solubor): 2.0 kg + 100 g / 1000 m² weekly.
  - Magnesium Sulphate: 1.0 kg / 1000 m² weekly.
- **Color Break & Harvesting Phase (Days 91 to 240):**
  - WSF 13:0:45 (Potassium Nitrate): 2.5 kg / 1000 m² every alternate day.
  - WSF 0:0:50 (SOP): 1.5 kg / 1000 m² twice a week for thick fruit pericarp, crispness, and vibrant fruit color.

## 3. Pruning & Canopy Architecture
- **Crown Flower Pruning:** Always remove the first crown flower appearing at the first fork division (Days 25-30) to allow robust vegetative framework development before fruit loading.
- **Shoot Training:** Maintain 4 main structural stems per plant; continuously prune all secondary lateral axillary suckers every 7-10 days.
