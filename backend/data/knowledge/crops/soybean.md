# Crop: Soybean (Glycine max)
- **Soil Suitability:** Deep, fertile Black clay loams and well-drained Alluvial loams (pH 6.5 - 7.5).
- **Fertilizer Recommendation:** N: 25 kg/ha (starter), P2O5: 60 kg/ha (Single Super Phosphate for Sulphur), K2O: 40 kg/ha.
- **Seed Inoculation:** Dual seed treatment with *Bradyrhizobium japonicum* + PSB @ 200g each per 10kg seed.
- **Pest Management:** Control Girdle Beetle and Spodoptera with Chlorantraniliprole 18.5% SC @ 60 ml/acre.
