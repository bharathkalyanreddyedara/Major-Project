# Comprehensive Agronomic Guide: Soybean (Glycine max)
*Authoritative Source: ICAR-IISR (Indian Institute of Soybean Research)*

## 1. Agro-Climatic & Soil Requirements
- **Optimal Soil:** Deep, well-drained Black clay soils (Vertisols) and fertile alluvial loams (pH 6.5 - 7.5).
- **Temperature & Water:** 25°C - 32°C; 600 - 800 mm rainfall. Highly sensitive to waterlogging at emergence.

## 2. Varieties & Sowing Protocols
- **Cultivars:** JS 335, JS 95-60, JS 20-34, NRC 37, NRC 86, RVS 2001-4, DSb 21.
- **Seed Rate:** 25-30 kg/acre (65-75 kg/ha). Spacing: 45x5 cm on Broad Bed Furrow (BBF).
- **Seed Inoculation:** Dual inoculation with *Bradyrhizobium japonicum* + PSB culture @ 200g/10kg seed.

## 3. Growth Stages & Nutrient Management
- **RDF (kg/ha):** 25:60:40:30 (N:P2O5:K2O:S). Full dose applied as basal using 375 kg Single Super Phosphate (SSP supplies Phosphorus + Sulphur).
- **Critical Stages:** Sowing, Flowering (35-45 DAS), and Pod development (60-70 DAS).

## 4. Integrated Pest Management
- **Girdle Beetle (*Obereopsis brevis*) & Tobacco Caterpillar (*Spodoptera litura*):**
  - *Control:* Spray Chlorantraniliprole 18.5% SC @ 60 ml/acre or Flubendiamide 39.35% SC @ 40 ml/acre.
- **Yellow Mosaic Virus (YMV):** Vector whiteflies controlled with Thiamethoxam 25% WG @ 40 g/acre.
