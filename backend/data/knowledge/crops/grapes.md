# Comprehensive Agronomic Guide: Grapes (Vitis vinifera)
*Authoritative Source: ICAR-NRCG (National Research Centre for Grapes)*

## 1. Soil, Climate & Training Systems
- **Soil:** Well-drained sandy loam, red loam, and light black soils (pH 6.5 - 8.0, EC < 1.5 dS/m).
- **Training System:** Bower / Y-trellis system for high light interception.
- **Cultivars:** Thompson Seedless, Manik Chaman, Sharad Seedless, Flame Seedless, Bangalore Blue, Cabernet Sauvignon (Wine).

## 2. Bi-Annual Pruning Schedule
- **Foundation Pruning (April):** Back pruning to 1-2 vegetative buds to develop healthy fruiting canes.
- **Fruit Pruning (October):** Forward pruning to 4-6 buds for cluster and flower initiation.

## 3. Fertigation & Growth Regulators
- **Gibberellic Acid (GA3):** Dipping clusters in 10-15 ppm GA3 for elongation at pre-bloom; 40 ppm GA3 for berry enlargement at 4-6 mm stage.
- **Water Soluble Nutrients:** 0:52:34 (MKP) and 13:0:45 (Potassium Nitrate) fed via drip for high Brix sweetness (> 18° Brix).

## 4. Disease Management
- **Downy Mildew (*Plasmopara viticola*):** Spray 1% Bordeaux mixture or Metalaxyl 8% + Mancozeb 64% WP @ 2.5 g/L.
- **Powdery Mildew (*Uncinula necator*):** Spray Azoxystrobin 23% SC @ 1 ml/L.
