# Comprehensive Agronomic Guide: Wheat (Triticum aestivum)
*Authoritative Source: ICAR-IIWBR (Indian Institute of Wheat & Barley Research)*

## 1. Agro-Climatic & Soil Requirements
- **Scientific Name:** *Triticum aestivum* L. (Family: Poaceae)
- **Optimal Soil Type:** Fertile, well-drained loams, silt loams, and alluvial soils.
- **Soil pH:** 6.0 - 7.5 (Moderately tolerant to neutral-alkaline soils).
- **Temperature Thresholds:** Sowing (20°C - 22°C), Tillering (15°C - 20°C), Heading & Flowering (20°C - 23°C), Ripening (25°C). Severe yield penalty if temperature > 32°C during grain filling (Terminal Heat Stress).
- **Water Requirement:** 450 - 550 mm total across 5-6 critical irrigations.

## 2. Varieties & Sowing Techniques
- **Popular Cultivars:** HD 2967, HD 3086 (Pusa Gautami), DBW 187 (Karan Vandana), DBW 222 (Karan Narendra), PBW 550, GW 322, HI 1544.
- **Seed Rate:** Timely Sown: 40 kg/acre (100 kg/ha); Late Sown / Broadcast: 50 kg/acre (125 kg/ha).
- **Seed Treatment:** Treat seed with Carboxin 37.5% + Thiram 37.5% WS @ 2.5g/kg seed + *Azotobacter* & PSB culture @ 200g/10kg seed.

## 3. Crop Growth Stages & Critical Irrigation Timings
### Stage 1: Crown Root Initiation - CRI (Day 1 - 25)
- **Critical Action:** **1st Irrigation at CRI (Day 20-25)** is the most crucial irrigation in wheat. Missing this reduces yield by 20-30%.
- **Basal Dose:** Full P (DAP 55 kg/acre) + full K (MOP 25 kg/acre) + 33% Nitrogen (Urea 30 kg/acre).
### Stage 2: Tillering & Jointing Phase (Day 26 - 55)
- **Field Actions:** 2nd Irrigation at Late Tillering (Day 40-45). Apply 1st Top Dressing: 33% Nitrogen (Urea @ 40 kg/acre).
- **Weed Control:** Apply Clodinafop-propargyl 15% WP @ 160 g/acre (for grassy weeds like *Phalaris minor*) + Metsulfuron-methyl 20% WP @ 8 g/acre (for broadleaf weeds) at 30-35 DAS.
### Stage 3: Booting, Heading & Flowering (Day 56 - 80)
- **Field Actions:** 3rd Irrigation at Late Jointing/Booting (Day 60-65) and 4th Irrigation at Flowering (Day 80-85). Apply remaining 33% Nitrogen.
- **Disease Watch:** Inspect lower leaves for Yellow Rust (*Puccinia striiformis*) stripe lesions.
### Stage 4: Milk & Soft Dough Stage (Day 81 - 105)
- **Field Actions:** 5th Irrigation at Milking (Day 95). Spray 1% Potassium Nitrate (KNO3) or 0.2% Zinc Sulphate to mitigate terminal heat stress.
### Stage 5: Hard Dough & Harvesting (Day 106 - 125)
- **Harvest Index:** Spikes turn golden yellow, straw dries, and grain moisture drops below 12%. Harvest with combine harvester.

## 4. Scientific Fertilizer Schedule (RDF: 120:60:40 N:P2O5:K2O kg/ha)
- **Basal:** 40 kg N (87 kg Urea) + 60 kg P2O5 (130 kg DAP) + 40 kg K2O (67 kg MOP)/ha.
- **1st Top-Dress (CRI Stage - 21 DAS):** 40 kg N (87 kg Urea/ha).
- **2nd Top-Dress (Jointing Stage - 45 DAS):** 40 kg N (87 kg Urea/ha).

## 5. Major Pests & Diseases
- **Yellow / Stripe Rust (*Puccinia striiformis*):**
  - *Symptoms:* Linear yellow stripe pustules parallel to leaf veins.
  - *Remedy:* Propiconazole 25% EC (Tilt) @ 200 ml/acre or Tebuconazole 25.9% EC @ 200 ml/acre in 200L water upon first sighting.
- **Loose Smut (*Ustilago tritici*):**
  - *Symptoms:* Entire spikelet transformed into black sooty spore mass.
  - *Prevention:* Seed treatment with Tebuconazole 2% DS @ 1.5g/kg seed.
- **Aphids (*Rhopalosiphum padi*):**
  - *Control:* Spray Thiamethoxam 25% WG @ 40 g/acre or Dimethoate 30% EC @ 300 ml/acre.
