# Crop: Wheat (Triticum aestivum)

## 1. Agro-Climatic Requirements & Soil Suitability
- **Optimal Soil Types:** Well-drained fertile Loam, Clay Loam, Alluvial Soil.
- **pH Range:** 6.0 - 7.5.
- **Temperature Thresholds:** Germination (20-25°C), Tillering (15-20°C), Grain Filling (20-23°C). Cool winter season (Rabi).
- **Water Requirement:** 450 - 550 mm total across 5-6 critical irrigations.

## 2. Growth Stages & Lifecycle Calendar (Sowing to Harvest: 110-125 Days)
### Stage 1: Sowing & Crown Root Initiation - CRI (Day 1 - 25)
- **Key Actions:** Treat seed with Trichoderma viride @ 4g/kg seed. Drill seed at 20 cm row spacing. **Critical 1st Irrigation at CRI stage (Day 21)**.
### Stage 2: Tillering & Jointing (Day 26 - 55)
- **Key Actions:** 1st Top Dressing: 50% Nitrogen (Urea @ 50 kg/acre). 2nd Irrigation at Late Tillering (Day 42). Apply Clodinafop-propargyl @ 160g/acre for Phalaris minor weed.
### Stage 3: Booting & Heading (Day 56 - 80)
- **Key Actions:** 3rd Irrigation at Booting (Day 65). 2nd Top Dressing: 25% Nitrogen (Urea). Monitor for Yellow/Brown Rust.
### Stage 4: Flowering & Milking (Day 81 - 100)
- **Key Actions:** 4th Irrigation at Flowering (Day 85). Spray Propiconazole 25% EC @ 200 ml/acre if Rust symptoms appear.
### Stage 5: Dough Stage & Harvest (Day 101 - 125)
- **Key Actions:** Final light irrigation at early dough stage. Harvest when straw turns golden yellow and grain is hard (moisture < 12%).

## 3. Recommended Fertilizers & Quantities (kg/ha)
- Nitrogen (N): 120 kg/ha (Urea: 260 kg/ha).
- Phosphorus (P2O5): 60 kg/ha (DAP: 130 kg/ha).
- Potassium (K2O): 40 kg/ha (MOP: 67 kg/ha).
