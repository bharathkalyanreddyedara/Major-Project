# Crop: Groundnut / Peanut (Arachis hypogaea)

## 1. Agro-Climatic Requirements & Soil Suitability
- **Optimal Soil Types:** Sandy loam, red sandy loam, light alluvial soils with loose friable texture for easy peg penetration.
- **pH Range:** 6.0 - 7.2.
- **Water Requirement:** 450 - 600 mm.

## 2. Growth Stages & Lifecycle Calendar (105-120 Days)
### Stage 1: Sowing & Vegetative (Day 1 - 30)
- **Actions:** Spacing 30x10 cm. Basal dose: 20 kg N + 40 kg P2O5 + 40 kg K2O + Gypsum @ 200 kg/ha.
### Stage 2: Flowering & Pegging (Day 31 - 55)
- **Actions:** **Critical Stage**: Apply 2nd split of Agricultural Gypsum @ 200 kg/ha at flowering (40-45 days) to supply Calcium for pod shell development and prevent "pops" (empty pods).
### Stage 3: Pod Development & Maturity (Day 56 - 110)
- **Actions:** Maintain optimal soil moisture for peg entry. Harvest when inside of shell turns dark brown/black.
