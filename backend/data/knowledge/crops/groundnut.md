# Comprehensive Agronomic Guide: Groundnut / Peanut (Arachis hypogaea)
*Authoritative Source: ICAR-DGR (Directorate of Groundnut Research)*

## 1. Agro-Climatic & Soil Requirements
- **Optimal Soil Type:** Well-drained sandy loam, red loams, and light alluvial soils with loose, friable tilth to facilitate peg entry and pod development.
- **Soil pH:** 6.0 - 7.5.
- **Temperature & Water:** 22°C - 30°C; 450 - 600 mm rainfall.

## 2. Varieties & Sowing Protocols
- **Popular Varieties:** Kadiri 6 (K6), Kadiri 9, TG 37A, TAG 24, GJG 9, JL 24 (Phule Pragati), TMV 2.
- **Seed Rate:** Bunch types: 40-50 kg kernels/acre (100-125 kg/ha); Spreading types: 35-40 kg/acre. Spacing: 30x10 cm.
- **Seed Treatment:** Treat kernels with Imidacloprid 600 FS @ 2 ml/kg seed (for white grub & thrips) + *Rhizobium* culture @ 200g/10kg kernels.

## 3. Growth Stages & Practices (105 - 120 Days)
### Stage 1: Emergence & Early Vegetative (Day 1 - 30)
- **Actions:** Basal dose: 20 kg N + 40 kg P2O5 + 40 kg K2O + 200 kg Gypsum/ha.
### Stage 2: Flowering & Peg Penetration (Day 31 - 55)
- **Critical Action:** **Apply 2nd split of Agricultural Gypsum @ 200 kg/ha at 40-45 DAS around plant base and incorporate lightly into soil**. Gypsum supplies Calcium directly to the subsurface pegs, preventing "pops" (empty pods) and increasing shelling percentage.
### Stage 3: Pod Development & Maturity (Day 56 - 110)
- **Actions:** Maintain optimal soil moisture for pegging. Withhold irrigation 7 days before lifting.
- **Maturity Index:** Inner shell wall turns dark brownish-black upon opening.

## 4. Fertilizer Schedule (RDF: 20:40:40 N:P2O5:K2O + 400 kg Gypsum/ha)
- Basal: 44 kg Urea + 250 kg SSP + 67 kg MOP + 200 kg Gypsum/ha.
- 45 DAS: 200 kg Gypsum/ha (top-dressed at flowering).

## 5. Major Pests & Diseases
- **Tikka Leaf Spot (*Cercospora personata* / *arachidicola*):**
  - *Symptoms:* Dark brown circular spots with yellow halos on leaves.
  - *Control:* Spray Hexaconazole 5% SC @ 2 ml/L or Tebuconazole 25.9% EC @ 1 ml/L.
- **White Grub (*Holotrichia consanguinea*):**
  - *Control:* Soil application of Fipronil 0.3% GR @ 10 kg/acre during sowing.
