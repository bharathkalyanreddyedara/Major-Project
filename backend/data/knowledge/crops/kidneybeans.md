# Comprehensive Agronomic Guide: Kidneybeans / Rajmash (Phaseolus vulgaris)
*Authoritative Source: ICAR-IIPR*

## 1. Soil & Unique Physiological Feature
- **Soil:** Deep, fertile, well-drained loams and mountain valley soils (pH 6.0 - 7.0).
- **Non-Nodulating Characteristic:** Unlike other pulse legumes, cultivated *Phaseolus vulgaris* lacks effective native *Rhizobium* root nodulation in Indian soils. **It requires full inorganic Nitrogen fertilization (100-120 kg N/ha) similar to cereal crops**.

## 2. Varieties & Sowing Protocols
- **Cultivars:** Hurra, PDR 14 (Uday), Amber, Utkarsh, Arun, HUR 15.
- **Seed Rate:** 35-40 kg/acre (85-100 kg/ha). Spacing: 40x10 cm. Sown in Rabi (Plains) or Kharif (Hills).

## 3. Fertilizer Schedule (RDF: 120:60:40 N:P2O5:K2O kg/ha)
- **Basal:** 40 kg N + 60 kg P2O5 + 40 kg K2O/ha.
- **1st Top-Dress (30 DAS):** 40 kg N (87 kg Urea/ha).
- **2nd Top-Dress (50 DAS - Pre-flowering):** 40 kg N (87 kg Urea/ha).
