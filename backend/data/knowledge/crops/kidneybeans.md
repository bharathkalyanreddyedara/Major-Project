# Crop: Kidneybeans / Rajmash (Phaseolus vulgaris)
- **Soil Suitability:** Deep, rich Loams and Mountain soils (pH 6.0 - 7.0).
- **Special Nitrogen Feature:** Unlike other pulses, non-nodulating *Phaseolus* requires full inorganic Nitrogen (N: 100-120 kg/ha in 3 splits).
- **Fertilizer:** N: 120 kg, P2O5: 60 kg, K2O: 40 kg/ha.
