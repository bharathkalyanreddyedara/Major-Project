# Strawberry Cultivation & Drip Fertigation Guide
*Authoritative Reference: AgricultureGuruji & ICAR-IARI Protocols*

## 1. Agro-Climatic Requirements
- **Climate:** Subtropical to temperate climate with mild winter days (18°C - 24°C) and cool nights (8°C - 13°C). Requires 7-8 hours of direct sunshine for high sugar accumulation (Brix > 8.5%).
- **Soil:** Rich sandy loam to loamy soil with excellent internal drainage, high organic matter (> 1.5%), and pH range of 5.5 - 6.5. EC must remain below 0.7 dS/m (highly salt sensitive).

## 2. Bed Preparation & Planting Architecture
- **Raised Beds:** 60 cm bed width, 30 cm height, 30 cm furrow.
- **Planting Geometry:** Zig-zag double row per bed with 30 cm row-to-row and 25-30 cm plant-to-plant spacing (Plant density: 22,000 - 25,000 plants/acre).
- **Mulching:** 25-micron black/silver mulch laid after bed preparation with drip laterals beneath. Hole punched for crown placement (crown must stay level with soil surface, never buried).

## 3. Water-Soluble Fertigation Schedule (Per Acre)
- **Establishment Stage (Days 1 to 20):**
  - WSF 12:61:0 (MAP): 2.5 kg/acre twice a week.
  - Humic acid (98% potassium humate): 500 g/acre once a week for rapid feeder root branching.
- **Vegetative & Crown Development (Days 21 to 45):**
  - WSF 19:19:19: 3.0 kg/acre every 3 days.
  - Magnesium Sulphate: 1.5 kg/acre weekly.
- **Flowering & Fruit Set (Days 46 to 75):**
  - WSF 0:52:34 (MKP): 3.5 kg/acre twice a week.
  - Calcium Nitrate: 2.5 kg/acre once a week (delivered separately via Tank A).
  - Boron (20% Solubor): 250 g/acre foliar spray at early flowering.
- **Fruit Sizing & Harvest Phase (Days 76 to 140):**
  - WSF 13:0:45 (Potassium Nitrate): 4.0 kg/acre twice a week.
  - WSF 0:0:50 (Potassium Sulphate - SOP): 2.0 kg/acre for berry shine, firmness, and shelf-life extension.

## 4. Pest & Disease Management
- **Red Spider Mite (*Tetranychus urticae*):** Abamectin 1.9% EC @ 1 ml/L or Propargite 57% EC @ 2 ml/L.
- **Botrytis Fruit Rot / Grey Mould:** Spray Azoxystrobin + Difenoconazole @ 1 ml/L or Trichoderma viride @ 5 g/L during dense flowering.
