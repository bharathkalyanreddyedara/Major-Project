# Comprehensive Agronomic Guide: Coconut (Cocos nucifera)
*Authoritative Source: ICAR-CPCRI (Central Plantation Crops Research Institute)*

## 1. Soil & Climate
- **Soil:** Coastal sandy, alluvial, red laterite, and loam soils with minimum 1.5m depth (pH 5.5 - 7.5).
- **Climate:** Tropical maritime climate (> 27°C, annual rainfall 1500 - 2500 mm).

## 2. Varieties & Spacing
- **Cultivars:** West Coast Tall (WCT), East Coast Tall, Chowghat Orange Dwarf (COD), TxD Hybrids (Kera Sankara, Chandrasankara).
- **Spacing:** 7.5 x 7.5m in square system (175 palms/ha).

## 3. Annual Fertilizer Schedule per Adult Palm
- 500g Nitrogen (1.1 kg Urea) + 320g P2O5 (2.0 kg Single Super Phosphate) + 1200g K2O (2.0 kg MOP) + 1.0 kg Magnesium Sulphate + 50 kg FYM applied in 2 splits (May-June and Sept-Oct).

## 4. Pest & Disease Management
- **Rhinoceros Beetle (*Oryctes rhinoceros*):** Place Naphthalene balls (3-4 balls) in leaf axils. Install Rhinolure aggregation traps.
- **Red Palm Weevil (*Rhynchophorus ferrugineus*):** Install Ferrolure traps; inject Imidacloprid (1 ml/L) into trunk boreholes.
