# Crop: Apple (Malus domestica)
- **Soil Suitability:** Deep, well-drained mountain clay loam rich in organic matter (pH 5.8 - 6.8).
- **Chilling Requirement:** 800 - 1200 hours below 7°C for bud dormancy break.
- **Nutrient Management:** FYM 50 kg + 700g N, 350g P2O5, 700g K2O per mature tree applied in December-January.
- **Disease Management:** Apple Scab (*Venturia inaequalis*) controlled with Mancozeb @ 0.3% and Difenoconazole @ 0.03%.
