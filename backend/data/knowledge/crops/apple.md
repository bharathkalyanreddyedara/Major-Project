# Comprehensive Agronomic Guide: Apple (Malus domestica)
*Authoritative Source: ICAR-CITH (Central Institute of Temperate Horticulture)*

## 1. Agro-Climatic Requirements
- **Elevation:** 1500 - 2700 metres above MSL.
- **Chilling Hours:** 800 - 1200 chilling hours below 7°C for breaking winter bud dormancy.
- **Soil:** Deep, well-drained mountain clay loam rich in organic matter (pH 5.8 - 6.8).

## 2. Rootstocks & Cultivars
- **High-Density Rootstocks:** M.9 (Dwarf), MM.106 (Semi-dwarf).
- **Cultivars:** Royal Delicious, Red Delicious, Gala, Fuji, Granny Smith, Super Chief.
- **Pollinators:** Plant 11-20% pollinizer varieties (Golden Delicious, Crab Apple) for cross-pollination by honeybees.

## 3. Annual Nutrient Management (per Mature Tree)
- 50 kg FYM + 700g N + 350g P2O5 + 700g K2O applied in root ring basin during winter dormancy (December-January).

## 4. Disease Management
- **Apple Scab (*Venturia inaequalis*):** Spray Mancozeb 75% WP @ 0.3% at green tip stage; Difenoconazole 25% EC @ 0.03% at pink bud and petal fall stages.
