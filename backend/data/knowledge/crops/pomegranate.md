# Comprehensive Agronomic Guide: Pomegranate (Punica granatum)
*Authoritative Source: ICAR-NRCP (National Research Centre on Pomegranate)*

## 1. Soil & Climate
- **Soil:** Well-drained light loams, alluvial, and medium black soils (pH 6.5 - 8.0).
- **Climate:** Semi-arid tropical to subtropical (25°C - 38°C). Dry hot climate during fruit maturation is essential for red aril color.

## 2. Bahar Regulation (Crop Flowering Management)
- **Ambe Bahar (Jan-Feb Flowering):** Water withheld in Dec; harvested July-Aug.
- **Mrig Bahar (June-July Flowering):** Water withheld in April-May; harvested Dec-Jan. (Preferred in semi-arid rainfed belts).
- **Hasta Bahar (Sept-Oct Flowering):** Water withheld in Aug; harvested March-April.

## 3. Fertilizer Schedule per Mature Plant (> 5 Years)
- 625g N + 250g P2O5 + 500g K2O + 40 kg FYM + 2 kg Neem cake + 20g Zinc Sulphate + 10g Boron.

## 4. Bacterial Blight / Telya (*Xanthomonas axonopodis* pv. *punicae*)
- **Symptoms:** Oily, water-soaked dark brown triangular spots on leaves and fruit skin; characteristic "L" or "Y" shaped cracks on fruit.
- **Management:** Prune infected twigs; spray 2-bromo-2-nitropropane-1,3-diol (Bronopol) @ 0.5 g/L + Copper Oxychloride @ 2.5 g/L.
