# Crop: Pomegranate (Punica granatum)
- **Soil Suitability:** Well-drained light loams and deep black soils (pH 6.5 - 8.0).
- **Bahar Treatment:** Regulate flowering season (Ambe Bahar, Mrig Bahar, or Hasta Bahar) by withholding water for 40-50 days.
- **Disease Management:** Bacterial Blight / Telya (*Xanthomonas axonopodis*) managed with 2-bromo-2-nitropropane-1,3-diol (Bronopol) @ 0.5g/L + Copper Oxychloride @ 2.5g/L.
