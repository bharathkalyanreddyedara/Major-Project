# Comprehensive Agronomic Guide: Sweet Orange & Mandarin (Citrus spp.)
*Authoritative Source: ICAR-CCRI (Central Citrus Research Institute)*

## 1. Soil & Climate
- **Soil:** Deep, well-aerated sandy loam and medium black vertisols (pH 6.5 - 7.5). Avoid saline soils (EC > 1.0 dS/m).
- **Climate:** Subtropical with distinct dry period to trigger winter/spring blossom.

## 2. Cultivars & Rootstocks
- **Cultivars:** Nagpur Mandarin (Santra), Mosambi (Sweet Orange), Coorg Mandarin, Kinnow.
- **Rootstocks:** Rangpur Lime (*Citrus limonia*) or Rough Lemon (*Citrus jambhiri*) for Phytophthora and drought tolerance.

## 3. Annual Fertilizer per Tree (> 7 Years)
- 600g N + 200g P2O5 + 400g K2O + 50 kg FYM + 250g Zinc Sulphate + 100g Borax.
- Foliar spray of 0.5% FeSO4 + 0.5% ZnSO4 + 0.2% Boric acid twice a year.

## 4. Pest & Disease Management
- **Citrus Psylla (*Diaphorina citri*):** Vectors Citrus Greening (HLB). Spray Thiamethoxam 25% WG @ 0.3 g/L.
- **Citrus Canker (*Xanthomonas citri*):** Spray Streptocycline 1g/10L + Copper Oxychloride 3g/L.
