# Crop: Sweet Orange & Mandarin (Citrus reticulata / sinensis)
- **Soil Suitability:** Deep, well-aerated sandy loam and light black soils (pH 6.5 - 7.5). Sensitive to high water tables.
- **Nutrient Management:** 600g N, 200g P2O5, 400g K2O + Zinc Sulphate (0.5%) and Ferrous Sulphate (0.5%) foliar sprays.
- **Disease Management:** Citrus Canker (*Xanthomonas*) controlled with Streptocycline (1g/10L) + Copper Oxychloride (3g/L); Citrus Gummosis controlled with Metalaxyl paste.
