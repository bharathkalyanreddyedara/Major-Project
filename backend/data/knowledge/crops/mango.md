# Crop: Mango (Mangifera indica)
- **Soil Suitability:** Deep alluvial or red loamy soil (depth > 2m) with good drainage (pH 5.5 - 7.5).
- **Fertilizer per Mature Tree:** 1000g N, 500g P2O5, 1000g K2O + 50 kg FYM applied in post-monsoon (September).
- **Pest & Disease:** Mango Hopper controlled with Imidacloprid (0.3 ml/L); Powdery Mildew controlled with Wettable Sulphur (2g/L) during flowering.
