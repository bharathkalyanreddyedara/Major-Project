# Comprehensive Agronomic Guide: Mango (Mangifera indica)
*Authoritative Source: ICAR-CISH (Central Institute for Subtropical Horticulture)*

## 1. Agro-Climatic Requirements
- **Soil:** Deep alluvial and red loamy soils (soil depth > 2 metres) with unobstructed drainage (pH 5.5 - 7.5).
- **Climate:** Tropical to subtropical with distinct dry season for floral induction.

## 2. High-Density Planting & Varieties
- **Commercial Cultivars:** Alphonso (Hapus), Kesar, Banganapalli, Dasheri, Langra, Amrapali (High Density: 2.5x2.5m - 1600 trees/ha), Mallika.
- **Pruning & Canopy:** Center-opening and annual shoot tip pruning immediately after harvest in July.

## 3. Nutrient Schedule per Bearing Tree (> 10 Years)
- 1000g Nitrogen + 500g P2O5 + 1000g K2O + 50 kg FYM + 250g Micronutrient mixture applied in September post-monsoon.
- Foliar spray of 0.2% Borax + 0.5% Potassium Nitrate at marble stage for fruit retention.

## 4. Pest & Disease Management
- **Mango Hopper (*Amritodus atkinsoni*):** Spray Imidacloprid 17.8% SL @ 0.3 ml/L or Thiamethoxam @ 0.3 g/L at panicle emergence.
- **Powdery Mildew (*Oidium mangiferae*):** Spray Wettable Sulphur 80% WP @ 2.5 g/L or Hexaconazole @ 1 ml/L during bloom.
