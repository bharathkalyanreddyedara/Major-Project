# Comprehensive Agronomic Guide: Mothbean (Vigna aconitifolia)
*Authoritative Source: ICAR-CAZRI (Central Arid Zone Research Institute)*

## 1. Soil & Extreme Arid Tolerance
- **Soil:** Light sandy and arid loamy sand soils (pH 7.0 - 8.5).
- **Climate:** Extreme heat and drought tolerance (up to 45°C). Requires only 200-350 mm rainfall.

## 2. Varieties & Sowing Parameters
- **Cultivars:** RMO 40, RMO 225, RMO 435, CZM 2, IPCMO 912.
- **Seed Rate:** 4 kg/acre (10 kg/ha). Spacing: 30x10 cm. Sown with onset of monsoon in Western Rajasthan and Gujarat.
- **Fertilizer:** Starter dose of 15 kg N + 40 kg P2O5/ha + *Rhizobium* seed inoculation.
