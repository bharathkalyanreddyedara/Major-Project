# Crop: Mothbean (Vigna aconitifolia)
- **Soil Suitability:** Arid and semi-arid light sandy soils (pH 7.0 - 8.5). Highest drought tolerance among Indian legumes.
- **Fertilizer:** 15 kg N + 40 kg P2O5/ha. Seed inoculation with Rhizobium.
- **Management:** Ideal for mixed cropping with Pearl Millet (Bajra) in western desert zones.
