# Crop: Sugarcane (Saccharum officinarum)

## 1. Agro-Climatic Requirements & Soil Suitability
- **Optimal Soil Types:** Deep fertile Loam, Alluvial, and well-drained Black Soil.
- **pH Range:** 6.5 - 8.0.
- **Temperature:** 25°C - 35°C; long warm growing season with sunshine.
- **Water Requirement:** 1500 - 2500 mm.

## 2. Lifecycle Calendar (Planted to Harvest: 10-12 Months)
### Stage 1: Germination & Sprouting (Month 1 - 2)
- **Actions:** Plant 2-3 budded setts treated with Carbendazim (1g/L). Basal application: 25% N + full P + 25% K.
### Stage 2: Tillering & Formative Phase (Month 3 - 4)
- **Actions:** 1st Top Dressing: 25% N. Earthing up at 90 days. Irrigate every 8-10 days.
### Stage 3: Grand Growth & Cane Elongation (Month 5 - 8)
- **Actions:** 2nd Top Dressing: Remaining 50% N + 75% K. Final heavy earthing up and trash mulching.
### Stage 4: Ripening & Maturity (Month 9 - 12)
- **Actions:** Brix reading > 18% with hand refractometer indicates peak sucrose maturity. Withhold irrigation 15 days before harvest.
