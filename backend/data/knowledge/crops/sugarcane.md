# Comprehensive Agronomic Guide: Sugarcane (Saccharum officinarum)
*Authoritative Source: ICAR-SBI (Sugarcane Breeding Institute)*

## 1. Agro-Climatic & Soil Requirements
- **Scientific Name:** *Saccharum officinarum* L. (Family: Poaceae)
- **Optimal Soil Type:** Deep fertile loams, alluvial soils, and well-drained clayey soils with minimum 1m rooting depth.
- **Soil pH:** 6.5 - 8.0.
- **Temperature & Sunlight:** 25°C - 35°C; requires 10-12 months of warm, sunny climate.
- **Water Requirement:** 1500 - 2500 mm.

## 2. Setts & Planting Technology
- **Popular Cultivars:** Co 0238 (Karan 4), Co 86032 (Nayana), Co 0118, CoLk 94184, CoC 671.
- **Planting Material:** 3-budded setts (35,000 - 40,000 setts/ha) or Single Bud Nursery Raised Seedlings (STP method).
- **Sett Treatment:** Dip setts in Carbendazim (1g/L) + Imidacloprid (1 ml/L) solution for 15 minutes before planting in furrows (90-120 cm row spacing).

## 3. Crop Growth Stages & Lifecycle Calendar (10 - 12 Months)
### Stage 1: Germination & Settling Phase (Month 1 - 2 / Day 1 - 60)
- **Actions:** Basal dose: 25% N + 100% P + 25% K. Irrigate every 7-10 days. Pre-emergence spray of Atrazine 50% WP @ 2 kg/ha.
### Stage 2: Tillering & Formative Phase (Month 3 - 4 / Day 61 - 120)
- **Actions:** 1st Top-Dress: 25% Nitrogen. Partial earthing up at 90 days. Spray Chlorpyrifos 20% EC @ 2 L/ha for Early Shoot Borer.
### Stage 3: Grand Growth & Cane Elongation (Month 5 - 8 / Day 121 - 240)
- **Actions:** 2nd Top-Dress: Remaining 50% N + 75% K. Heavy final earthing up to prevent lodging. Detrashing of lower dried leaves to improve aeration.
### Stage 4: Ripening & Sugar Accumulation (Month 9 - 12 / Day 241 - 360)
- **Actions:** Hand refractometer Brix reading > 18% indicates peak maturity. Withhold irrigation 15-20 days prior to harvest.

## 4. Fertilizer Schedule (RDF: 250:100:120 N:P2O5:K2O kg/ha)
- **Basal:** 62.5 kg N + 100 kg P2O5 + 30 kg K2O + 50 kg Zinc Sulphate + 25 tonnes FYM/ha.
- **45 Days:** 62.5 kg N/ha.
- **90 Days (Formative):** 62.5 kg N + 45 kg K2O/ha.
- **120 Days (Grand Growth):** 62.5 kg N + 45 kg K2O/ha.

## 5. Major Pests & Diseases
- **Early Shoot Borer (*Chilo infuscatellus*):** Dead hearts in young shoots. Apply Cartap Hydrochloride 4G @ 25 kg/ha in furrows.
- **Red Rot (*Colletotrichum falcatum*):** Yellowing of crown, reddening of internal cane pith with white transverse bands. Prevent via resistant varieties (Co 86032) and sett treatment.
