# Comprehensive Agronomic Guide: Sorghum / Jowar (Sorghum bicolor)
*Authoritative Source: ICAR-IIMR (Indian Institute of Millets Research)*

## 1. Agro-Climatic & Soil Requirements
- **Optimal Soil Type:** Deep black clayey soils (Vertisols) with high water holding capacity for Rabi sorghum; medium black and loamy soils for Kharif.
- **Soil pH:** 6.0 - 8.5 (Tolerates moderate salinity and alkalinity).
- **Temperature:** 26°C - 34°C (Susceptible to frost; germination requires min 15°C).
- **Rainfall:** 400 - 750 mm (Extremely drought hardy).

## 2. Certified Varieties & Sowing
- **Kharif Hybrids:** CSH 14, CSH 16, CSH 25, CSH 30, CSV 15.
- **Rabi Varieties:** CSV 216R, CSV 22R, CSV 29R, Phule Yashoda, Maldandi (M 35-1).
- **Seed Rate:** 7.5 - 8.0 kg/ha.
- **Seed Treatment:** Treat with *Trichoderma viride* @ 4 g/kg seed + Imidacloprid 70 WS @ 3 g/kg seed (for shoot fly control) + *Azospirillum* biofertilizer.
- **Spacing:** 45 cm row-to-row and 12-15 cm plant-to-plant (Plant population: 150,000 plants/ha).

## 3. Growth Stages & Agronomic Calendar (100 - 120 Days)
- **Emergence & Seedling Establishment (1-20 DAS):** Critical window for shoot fly scouting. Dead hearts appear on 1-4 week old seedlings.
- **Knee-High & Flag Leaf (20-50 DAS):** Inter-cultivation and earthing up. First top-dressing of Nitrogen.
- **Booting & Flowering (50-75 DAS):** Critical water sensitive period. Provide protective irrigation if monsoon breaks.
- **Grain Filling & Hard Dough (75-100 DAS):** Inspect for grain mold during rainy harvest.
- **Maturity & Harvesting (105-120 DAS):** Harvest when black layer forms at the base of the grain (physiological maturity). Moisture below 12% for storage.

## 4. Nutrient Management
- **Target NPK:** 80 kg N : 40 kg P2O5 : 40 kg K2O per hectare.
- **Basal:** 50% N + 100% P + 100% K at sowing.
- **Top-dressing:** 50% N (Urea) at 30-35 DAS before second irrigation/hoeing.

## 5. Pest & Disease Management
- **Sorghum Shoot Fly (*Atherigona soccata*):** Complete sowing within 10-15 days of monsoon onset. Spray Chlorantraniliprole 18.5 SC @ 0.3 ml/L or Thiamethoxam 25 WG @ 0.5 g/L if >10% dead hearts occur.
- **Stem Borer (*Chilo partellus*):** Whorl application of Carbofuran 3G @ 8 kg/ha at 25-30 DAS.
- **Grain Mold (*Fusarium moniliforme*):** Spray Propiconazole 25 EC @ 1 ml/L at 50% flowering if unseasonal rains occur.
