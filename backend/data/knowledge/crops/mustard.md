# Comprehensive Agronomic Guide: Indian Mustard (Brassica juncea)
*Authoritative Source: ICAR-DRMR (Directorate of Rapeseed-Mustard Research)*

## 1. Agro-Climatic & Soil Requirements
- **Soil:** Light to heavy loams, alluvial, and sandy loam soils with good drainage (pH 6.0 - 7.5).
- **Season & Climate:** Rabi season crop (15°C - 25°C). Requires clear sunny weather during flowering.

## 2. Varieties & Sowing Parameters
- **Cultivars:** Pusa Bold, Pusa Jai Kisan (Bio 902), Giriraj, RH 749, NRCHB 101, DRMRIJ 31.
- **Seed Rate:** 1.5 - 2.0 kg/acre (3.5 - 5.0 kg/ha). Spacing: 30-45 cm x 10-15 cm. Sowing depth: 2-3 cm.

## 3. Scientific Fertilizer Schedule (RDF: 80:40:40 N:P2O5:K2O + 30 S kg/ha)
- **Basal:** 40 kg N + 40 kg P2O5 + 40 kg K2O + **30 kg Elemental Sulphur/ha** (Sulphur increases oil percentage by 2-3%).
- **Top-Dress (30 DAS at 1st Irrigation):** 40 kg N (87 kg Urea/ha).

## 4. Pest & Disease Management
- **Mustard Aphid (*Lipaphis erysimi*):**
  - *Damage:* Colonies suck sap from tender inflorescence, causing curl and pod distortion.
  - *ETL:* 1.5-2.0 cm aphid colony on central shoot.
  - *Control:* Spray Dimethoate 30% EC @ 1.7 ml/L or Thiamethoxam 25% WG @ 0.4 g/L.
- **White Rust (*Albugo candida*):**
  - *Symptoms:* White porcelain-like pustules on leaves; "Stag head" floral malformation.
  - *Control:* Seed treatment with Metalaxyl 35% WS @ 6g/kg seed + foliar spray of Metalaxyl-Mancozeb @ 2g/L.
