# Crop: Mustard / Rapeseed (Brassica juncea)
- **Soil Suitability:** Light to heavy Loams and Alluvial soils (pH 6.0 - 7.5).
- **Fertilizer Recommendation:** N: 80 kg/ha, P2O5: 40 kg/ha, K2O: 40 kg/ha + **Elemental Sulphur @ 30 kg/ha** (crucial for glucosinolate oil synthesis).
- **Pest Management:** Mustard Aphids (Lipaphis erysimi) controlled with Dimethoate 30% EC @ 1.7 ml/L or Thiamethoxam 25% WG @ 0.4 g/L.
