# Crop: Watermelon (Citrullus lanatus)
- **Soil Suitability:** Sandy loam and riverbed alluvial soils with good drainage (pH 6.0 - 7.0).
- **Fertigation:** N: 100 kg, P2O5: 60 kg, K2O: 100 kg/ha. Shift to high Potassium during fruit expansion for high Brix sweetness.
- **Maturity Index:** Dull metallic thud sound upon tapping; ground spot turns creamy yellow; tendril nearest to fruit dries up.
