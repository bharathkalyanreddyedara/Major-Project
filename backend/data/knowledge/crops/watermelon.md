# Comprehensive Agronomic Guide: Watermelon (Citrullus lanatus)
*Authoritative Source: ICAR-IIVR (Indian Institute of Vegetable Research)*

## 1. Soil & Climate
- **Soil:** Sandy loam, riverbed alluvial loams with loose texture and excellent drainage (pH 6.0 - 7.0).
- **Climate:** Warm, sunny, dry climate (24°C - 32°C). High humidity during ripening causes foliar fungal blights.

## 2. Sowing & Drip Fertigation
- **Hybrids:** Sugar Baby, Black Beauty, Maxx, Pusa Bedana (Seedless), Kiran, Vishala.
- **Seed Rate:** 300 - 400 g/acre. Spacing: 2.0 - 2.5m row spacing x 0.6m plant spacing on raised beds with silver-black mulch.
- **Fertigation (RDF):** N: 100 kg, P2O5: 60 kg, K2O: 100 kg/ha. Increase Potassium fertigation (0:0:50) during fruit enlargement for sweet red flesh.

## 3. Harvest Indices
- Tendril adjacent to the fruit stem completely withers and dries up.
- Ground spot (bottom where fruit touches soil) turns from white to creamy yellow.
- Tapping the fruit produces a dull, hollow thud (metallic sound indicates immaturity).
