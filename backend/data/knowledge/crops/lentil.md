# Crop: Lentil / Masoor (Lens culinaris)
- **Soil Suitability:** Fertile Loams and Alluvial soils of Gangetic plains (pH 6.0 - 7.5).
- **Fertilizer:** N: 20 kg, P2O5: 40 kg, K2O: 20 kg + 15 kg Sulphur/ha.
- **Disease Management:** Lentil Wilt (*Fusarium*) and Rust managed with seed treatment (Carbendazim) and Hexaconazole spray.
