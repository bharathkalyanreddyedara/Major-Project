# Comprehensive Agronomic Guide: Lentil / Masoor (Lens culinaris)
*Authoritative Source: ICAR-IIPR*

## 1. Soil & Season
- **Soil:** Silt loam, clay loam, and alluvial soils of Indo-Gangetic plains (pH 6.0 - 7.5).
- **Season:** Rabi pulse (Cool winter season; 12°C - 25°C).

## 2. Varieties & Sowing Protocols
- **Cultivars:** Pusa Ageti, DPL 62 (Sheri), IPL 81, HUL 57, KLS 218.
- **Seed Rate:** Small seeded: 12-14 kg/acre (30-35 kg/ha); Bold seeded: 16-18 kg/acre (40-45 kg/ha).
- **Fertilizer (RDF):** 20 kg N + 40 kg P2O5 + 20 kg K2O + 15 kg S/ha.
- **Disease Management:** Lentil Wilt (*Fusarium*) controlled with *Trichoderma* seed treatment @ 4g/kg seed.
