# Comprehensive Agronomic Guide: Millets & Ragi (Eleusine coracana / Pennisetum glaucum)
*Authoritative Source: ICAR-IIMR (Indian Institute of Millets Research)*

## 1. Agro-Climatic & Soil Requirements
- **Optimal Soil Type:** Red loams, sandy loams, light black soils, and lateritic soils with good surface drainage. Tolerates marginal poor-fertility soils.
- **Soil pH:** 5.0 - 8.2 (Highly tolerant to slightly acidic and sodic conditions).
- **Temperature:** 24°C - 35°C (C4 photosynthesis plant with exceptional water-use efficiency).
- **Rainfall:** 350 - 600 mm (Drought-resilient climate-smart crop).

## 2. Certified Varieties & Sowing
- **Finger Millet (Ragi):** GPU 28, GPU 48, ML 365, Indaf 9, KMR 301.
- **Pearl Millet (Bajra):** HHB 67 Improved, RHB 177, GHB 558, MPMH 17.
- **Seed Rate:** Direct drilling: 8-10 kg/ha; Transplanting (Ragi): 4-5 kg/ha.
- **Seed Treatment:** Inoculate with *Azospirillum brasilense* (500 g/ha) + PSB (500 g/ha) + *Trichoderma* (4 g/kg seed).
- **Spacing:** 22.5 cm x 10 cm for direct sowing; 30 cm x 10 cm for Bajra.

## 3. Growth Stages & Crop Schedule (85 - 105 Days)
- **Nursery / Sowing (1-18 DAS):** Shallow sowing at 2-3 cm depth. Light harrowing after first rain.
- **Active Tillering (20-40 DAS):** Hand weeding or inter-cultivation with blade harrow at 25 DAS. First top-dressing of Nitrogen.
- **Earhead Emergence / Flag Leaf (45-65 DAS):** Critical moisture sensitive stage. If drought occurs, provide one life-saving protective irrigation.
- **Grain Filling & Dough Stage (65-85 DAS):** Protect from bird damage.
- **Maturity & Harvesting (85-100 DAS):** Harvest when earheads turn reddish-brown (Ragi) or golden-yellow (Bajra) and grain moisture reaches 14%.

## 4. Fertilizer Management
- **Target NPK:** 60 kg N : 30 kg P2O5 : 30 kg K2O per hectare + 5 tonnes FYM.
- **Basal:** 50% N + 100% P + 100% K at time of land preparation.
- **Top-dressing:** 50% N (Urea) at 25-30 DAS after weeding.

## 5. Pest & Disease Management
- **Ragi Blast (*Pyricularia grisea*):** Spray Tricyclazole 75% WP @ 0.6 g/L or Kasugamycin 3% SL @ 1.5 ml/L at tillering and neck emergence.
- **Shoot Fly & Stem Borer (*Atherigona soccata*):** Sowing within 10 days of monsoon onset. Seed treatment with Imidacloprid 70 WS @ 5 g/kg seed.
- **Downy Mildew / Green Ear Disease (Bajra):** Spray Metalaxyl-M 31.8% ES seed dressing @ 2 ml/kg seed.
