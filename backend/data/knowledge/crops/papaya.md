# Comprehensive Agronomic Guide: Papaya (Carica papaya)
*Authoritative Source: ICAR-IIHR*

## 1. Soil & Climate
- **Soil:** Well-drained alluvial and sandy loam soils with high drainage. Extremely intolerant to water stagnation (even 24h waterlogging causes root collapse).
- **pH:** 6.0 - 7.0. Temperature: 22°C - 35°C.

## 2. Cultivars & Spacing
- **Gynodioecious Cultivars (All plants productive):** Red Lady 786, Solo, Coorg Honey Dew, Arka Prabhat.
- **Spacing:** 1.8 x 1.8m or 2.1 x 2.1m (2500 - 3000 plants/ha).

## 3. Fertigation Schedule per Plant/Year
- 200g N + 200g P2O5 + 250g K2O delivered in bimonthly splits + 20 kg FYM.
- Spray 0.1% Boron at flowering to prevent fruit deformation and lumpy skin.

## 4. Disease Management
- **Papaya Ring Spot Virus (PRSV):** Transmitted by aphids. Intercrop with border rows of corn/sorghum and use silver reflective mulch.
- **Collar Rot / Stem Rot (*Pythium aphanidermatum*):** Drench soil basin with Metalaxyl-Mancozeb @ 2.5 g/L.
