# Crop: Papaya (Carica papaya)
- **Soil Suitability:** Well-drained, rich alluvial and sandy loam soils. Extreme susceptibility to collar rot under water stagnation.
- **Fertigation:** 200g N, 200g P2O5, 250g K2O per plant per year applied in 6 bimonthly splits.
- **Disease Management:** Papaya Ring Spot Virus (PRSV) vector aphids managed with systemic insecticides and silver reflective mulching.
