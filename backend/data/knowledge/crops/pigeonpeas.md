# Comprehensive Agronomic Guide: Pigeonpea / Red Gram (Cajanus cajan)
*Authoritative Source: ICAR-IIPR*

## 1. Soil & Climate
- **Soil:** Deep, well-drained medium-to-heavy Black soils and alluvial loams (pH 6.5 - 8.0).
- **Season:** Kharif crop with long taproot system enabling high drought tolerance.

## 2. Varieties & Sowing Protocols
- **Cultivars:** Asha (ICPL 87119), Maruti (ICP 8863), BSMR 736, BDN 711, Pusa 992.
- **Seed Rate:** Pure crop: 4-5 kg/acre (10-12 kg/ha); Intercropped with Cotton/Soybean (1:3 or 1:4 ratio): 2 kg/acre. Spacing: 90-120 x 20-30 cm.
- **Seed Inoculation:** *Rhizobium* + PSB culture @ 200g/10kg seed.

## 3. Fertilizer Schedule (RDF: 20:50:20 N:P2O5:K2O + 20 S kg/ha)
- Full dose applied as basal: 44 kg Urea + 312 kg SSP + 33 kg MOP/ha.

## 4. Pest Management
- **Pod Borer Complex (*Helicoverpa armigera* & *Maruca vitrata*):**
  - *Control:* Spray Flubendiamide 39.35% SC @ 40 ml/acre or Chlorantraniliprole @ 60 ml/acre at 50% flowering.
