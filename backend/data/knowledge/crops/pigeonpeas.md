# Crop: Pigeonpea / Red Gram (Cajanus cajan)
- **Soil Suitability:** Deep well-drained Loamy and Black soils (pH 6.5 - 8.0).
- **Fertilizer:** N: 20 kg (Starter), P2O5: 50 kg, K2O: 20 kg + 20 kg Sulphur/ha.
- **Pest Management:** Helicoverpa pod borer and Maruca spotted pod borer controlled with Flubendiamide 39.35% SC @ 40 ml/acre.
