# Crop: Cotton (Gossypium hirsutum)

## 1. Agro-Climatic Requirements & Soil Suitability
- **Optimal Soil Types:** Deep Black Cotton Soils (Vertisols), fertile Alluvial Loam.
- **pH Range:** 6.5 - 8.2.
- **Temperature Thresholds:** 21°C - 35°C; requires minimum 180-200 frost-free days.
- **Water Requirement:** 700 - 1000 mm seasonal water.

## 2. Growth Stages & Lifecycle Calendar (Sowing to Harvest: 150-180 Days)
### Stage 1: Sowing & Emergence (Day 1 - 20)
- **Key Actions:** Dibble seeds at 90x60 cm or 120x45 cm spacing. Basal application: 25% N + full P + 50% K.
### Stage 2: Vegetative & Square Formation (Day 21 - 60)
- **Key Actions:** Earthing up at 45 days. 1st Top Dressing: 35% Nitrogen (Urea). Spray 1% Magnesium Sulphate (MgSO4) to prevent red leaf disease.
### Stage 3: Flowering & Boll Development (Day 61 - 110)
- **Key Actions:** 2nd Top Dressing: 25% Nitrogen + remaining 50% Potash. Spray Planofix (NAA) @ 4 ml/15L to prevent square/boll shedding.
### Stage 4: Boll Maturation & Bursting (Day 111 - 150)
- **Key Actions:** Spray 1% Potassium Nitrate (KNO3) for boll weight and fibre strength. Monitor for Pink Bollworm.
### Stage 5: Picking / Harvest (Day 151 - 180)
- **Key Actions:** Pick clean, fully opened bolls in dry morning hours. Keep seed cotton free of bracts and trash.

## 3. Recommended Fertilizers & Quantities (kg/ha)
- Nitrogen (N): 120-150 kg/ha.
- Phosphorus (P2O5): 60 kg/ha.
- Potassium (K2O): 60 kg/ha.
- Magnesium Sulphate: 25 kg/ha.
