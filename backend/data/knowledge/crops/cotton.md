# Comprehensive Agronomic Guide: Cotton (Gossypium hirsutum)
*Authoritative Source: ICAR-CICR (Central Institute for Cotton Research)*

## 1. Agro-Climatic & Soil Requirements
- **Scientific Name:** *Gossypium hirsutum* L. / *Gossypium arboreum* (Family: Malvaceae)
- **Optimal Soil Type:** Deep black cotton soils (Vertisols) and fertile alluvial loams with good subsurface drainage.
- **Soil pH:** 6.5 - 8.2 (Tolerates moderate salinity).
- **Temperature Thresholds:** Optimal 21°C - 35°C; requires a minimum of 180-200 frost-free days with bright sunshine.
- **Water Requirement:** 700 - 1000 mm seasonal water.

## 2. Hybrids & Sowing Parameters
- **Popular Hybrids & Bt Cultivars:** RCH 659 BG-II, DCH 32, Bunny Bt, Mallika Bt, PKV 081, Suraj.
- **Seed Rate:** Hybrids / Bt Cotton: 1.5 - 2.0 kg/acre (dibbled at 90x60 cm or 120x45 cm); Desi Cotton: 5-6 kg/acre.
- **Seed Treatment:** Treat non-Bt seeds with Imidacloprid 70% WS @ 5g/kg seed for sucking pest protection for first 30 days + *Trichoderma viride* @ 4g/kg.

## 3. Crop Growth Stages & Lifecycle Calendar (150 - 180 Days)
### Stage 1: Germination & Seedling Establishment (Day 1 - 25)
- **Field Actions:** Dibble seeds at 3-5 cm depth. Basal dose: 25% N + full P + 50% K.
- **Weed Control:** Apply Pendimethalin 30% EC @ 1.0 L/acre as pre-emergence spray within 48 hours of sowing.
### Stage 2: Square Formation & Branching (Day 26 - 60)
- **Field Actions:** Earthing up at 45 DAS. 1st Top-Dressing: 35% Nitrogen (Urea).
- **Nutrient Spray:** Spray 1% Magnesium Sulphate (MgSO4) + 1% Urea to prevent "Red leaf disease" (reddening of leaves).
### Stage 3: Flowering & Boll Setting Phase (Day 61 - 110)
- **Field Actions:** 2nd Top-Dressing: Remaining 40% Nitrogen + remaining 50% Potash.
- **Hormone Spray:** Spray Planofix (NAA 4.5% SL) @ 4 ml per 15L water at 60 and 75 DAS to prevent flower bud and square shedding.
### Stage 4: Boll Development & Maturation (Day 111 - 150)
- **Field Actions:** Spray 1% Potassium Nitrate (13-0-45) or 19-19-19 twice during peak boll swelling to increase boll weight and staple fibre length.
- **Protection:** Monitor for Pink Bollworm entering bolls.
### Stage 5: Bursting & Picking Phase (Day 151 - 180)
- **Harvest:** Pick clean, fully opened fluffy seed cotton in morning hours after dew evaporates. Store in clean cotton bags.

## 4. Scientific Fertilizer Schedule (RDF: 150:60:60 N:P2O5:K2O kg/ha)
- **Basal:** 35 kg N (75 kg Urea) + 60 kg P2O5 (130 kg DAP or 375 kg SSP) + 30 kg K2O (50 kg MOP)/ha.
- **1st Split (Square Formation - 45 DAS):** 55 kg N (120 kg Urea/ha).
- **2nd Split (Peak Flowering - 75 DAS):** 60 kg N (130 kg Urea) + 30 kg K2O (50 kg MOP)/ha.

## 5. Major Pests & Integrated Pest Management
- **Whitefly (*Bemisia tabaci*) & Jassids (*Amrasca biguttula*):**
  - *Damage:* Sucking sap causing leaf curling, hopper burn, and transmission of Cotton Leaf Curl Virus (CLCuD).
  - *Control:* Install yellow sticky traps @ 25/acre. Spray Diafenthiuron 50% WP @ 240 g/acre or Flonicamid 50% WG @ 60 g/acre or Pyriproxyfen 10% EC @ 400 ml/acre.
- **Pink Bollworm (*Pectinophora gossypiella*):**
  - *Symptoms:* "Rosetted flowers", locked bolls, stained lint.
  - *ETL:* 8 moths/trap/night for 3 consecutive days or 10% damaged bolls.
  - *Control:* Pheromone traps with Gossyplure @ 10/acre. Spray Emamectin Benzoate 5% SG @ 100 g/acre or Chlorantraniliprole 18.5% SC @ 60 ml/acre or Spinetoram 11.7% SC @ 170 ml/acre.
