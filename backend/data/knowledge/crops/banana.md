# Comprehensive Agronomic Guide: Banana (Musa acuminata / paradisiaca)
*Authoritative Source: ICAR-NRCB (National Research Centre for Banana)*

## 1. Soil & Climatic Parameters
- **Soil:** Deep, well-drained loamy soil rich in organic matter (pH 6.5 - 7.5). High drainage is mandatory.
- **Climate:** Tropical warm humid climate (15°C - 35°C); 1800 - 2200 mm water requirement.

## 2. Planting & Fertigation Schedule
- **Cultivars:** Grand Naine (G9), Robusta, Dwarf Cavendish, Rasthali, Nendran, Red Banana.
- **Planting Material:** Disease-free tissue culture plantlets or sword suckers (1.5-2.0 kg). Spacing: 1.8x1.8m or paired row 1.2x1.2x2.0m.
- **Nutrient Requirement per Plant:** 200g N, 50g P2O5, 300g K2O + 20 kg FYM. Delivered through drip fertigation in 30 weekly splits from 2nd to 8th month.

## 3. Disease Management
- **Panama Wilt (*Fusarium oxysporum* f. sp. *cubense* TR4):** Soil drenching with *Trichoderma viride* + Carbendazim 0.1%.
- **Sigatoka Leaf Spot (*Mycosphaerella musicola*):** Spray Propiconazole 25% EC (1 ml/L) or Mineral oil (1%).
