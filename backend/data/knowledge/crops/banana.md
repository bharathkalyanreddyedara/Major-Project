# Crop: Banana (Musa acuminata)
- **Soil Suitability:** Deep, rich loamy soil with high drainage (pH 6.5 - 7.5). Avoid saline soils.
- **Nutrient Requirement:** Heavy feeder. N: 200-250g, P2O5: 50g, K2O: 300g per plant applied via drip fertigation.
- **Disease Management:** Panama Wilt (*Fusarium oxysporum*) controlled with Carbendazim injection; Sigatoka leaf spot managed with Propiconazole (1 ml/L).
