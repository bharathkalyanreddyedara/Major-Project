# Crop: Tomato (Solanum lycopersicum)

## 1. Agro-Climatic Requirements & Soil Suitability
- **Optimal Soil Types:** Well-drained sandy loam to clay loam rich in organic matter.
- **pH Range:** 6.0 - 7.0.
- **Temperature:** 20°C - 28°C.

## 2. Lifecycle & Key Management (90-130 Days)
- **Nutrient Doses:** N: 150 kg, P2O5: 100 kg, K2O: 120 kg/ha + Boron foliar spray (0.1% Solubor) at flowering.
- **Staking:** Stake indeterminate plants with bamboo poles at 30 DAT to keep fruit off wet soil.
- **Pest Control:** Install pheromone traps for Tuta absoluta and Helicoverpa fruit borer.
