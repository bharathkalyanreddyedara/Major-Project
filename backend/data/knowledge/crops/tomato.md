# Comprehensive Agronomic Guide: Tomato (Solanum lycopersicum)
*Authoritative Source: ICAR-IIHR (Indian Institute of Horticultural Research)*

## 1. Agro-Climatic & Soil Requirements
- **Optimal Soil Type:** Well-drained fertile sandy loam to clay loam rich in organic carbon.
- **Soil pH:** 6.0 - 7.0.
- **Temperature:** 20°C - 28°C (Night temperature > 15°C is essential for fruit set).

## 2. Hybrids & Sowing Parameters
- **Popular Hybrids:** Arka Rakshak (Triple disease resistant: ToLCV + BW + EB), Arka Samrat, Pusa Ruby, US 440, Syngenta Abhinav, Lakshmi.
- **Seed Rate:** Hybrids: 50-60 g/acre; Varieties: 150-200 g/acre.
- **Spacing:** 90x60 cm or 120x45 cm on raised beds with drip irrigation and silver-black plastic mulch (25 micron).

## 3. Growth Stages & Practices (90 - 130 Days)
- **Nursery (1-25 Days):** Raise seedlings in 98-cell pro-trays using sterilized coco-peat + *Trichoderma*.
- **Transplanting (25-30 Days):** Drench seedling roots in Imidacloprid (0.5 ml/L) before field planting.
- **Staking:** Stake indeterminate plants with bamboo trellises at 30 DAT.
- **Fertigation Schedule:** Total N: 150 kg, P2O5: 100 kg, K2O: 150 kg/ha applied through drip in weekly splits using 19:19:19, 0:52:34, and 13:0:45. Spray 0.1% Boron + 0.5% Calcium Nitrate during flowering to prevent Blossom End Rot.

## 4. Pest & Disease Management
- **Tomato Leaf Curl Virus (ToLCV):** Transmitted by Whitefly (*Bemisia tabaci*). Control vectors with Cyantraniliprole 10.26% OD @ 1.8 ml/L.
- **Early Blight & Late Blight:** Spray Azoxystrobin 18.2% + Difenoconazole 11.4% SC @ 1 ml/L or Metalaxyl 8% + Mancozeb 64% WP @ 2.5 g/L.
- **Pinworm (*Tuta absoluta*):** Install pheromone traps @ 16/acre. Spray Chlorantraniliprole 18.5% SC @ 0.4 ml/L.
