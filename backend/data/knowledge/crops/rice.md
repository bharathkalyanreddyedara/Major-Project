# Comprehensive Agronomic Guide: Rice (Oryza sativa)
*Authoritative Source: ICAR-NRRI (National Rice Research Institute) & TNAU Agritech Portal*

## 1. Agro-Climatic & Soil Requirements
- **Scientific Name:** *Oryza sativa* L. (Family: Poaceae)
- **Optimal Soil Type:** Clay loams, heavy alluvial soils, and clayey vertisols with low percolation rates and high water-retention capacity.
- **Soil pH:** 5.5 - 7.5 (Tolerates slight soil acidity and sodicity).
- **Temperature Requirements:** Germination (20°C - 35°C), Tillering (25°C - 31°C), Flowering (26°C - 30°C). Sensitive to temperatures < 15°C (causes spikelet sterility).
- **Rainfall & Water Need:** 1100 - 1500 mm total seasonal water.

## 2. High-Yielding Varieties & Seed Preparation
- **Popular HYVs & Hybrids:** MTU 1010 (Cottondora Sannalu), BPT 5204 (Samba Mahsuri), IR 64, Swarna (MTU 7029), Pusa Basmati 1121, Pusa Basmati 1509, CR Dhan 310 (High Protein), DRR Dhan 45 (Zinc Rich).
- **Seed Rate:** Transplanted puddled rice: 20-25 kg/acre (50-60 kg/ha); Direct Seeded Rice (DSR): 10-12 kg/acre; SRI System: 2 kg/acre.
- **Seed Treatment:** Soak seeds in 1% Salt solution to eliminate chaffy seeds. Treat with Carbendazim 50% WP @ 2g/kg seed + *Pseudomonas fluorescens* @ 10g/kg seed 24 hours before nursery sowing.

## 3. Crop Growth Stages & Lifecycle Calendar (120 - 135 Days)
### Stage 1: Nursery Phase & Seedling Establishment (Day 1 - 25)
- **Field Actions:** Raise wet bed nursery (1000 m² for 1 ha main field). Apply 10 kg DAP + 5 kg Urea in nursery.
- **Growth Marker:** Seedlings attain 4-5 leaves (15-20 cm height) by 21-25 days.
### Stage 2: Mainfield Puddling & Transplanting (Day 26 - 40)
- **Field Actions:** Puddle field 2-3 times to create impermeable plow-sole layer. Transplant 2-3 seedlings/hill at 20x15 cm spacing.
- **Basal Dose:** Apply 25% Nitrogen + 100% Phosphorus (DAP/SSP) + 50% Potash (MOP) + 25 kg/ha Zinc Sulphate.
- **Weed Control:** Apply Pretilachlor 50% EC @ 500 ml/acre or Pyrazosulfuron-ethyl 10% WP @ 80 g/acre within 3 days of transplanting in 2-3 cm standing water.
### Stage 3: Active Tillering & Panicle Primordium Initiation (Day 41 - 70)
- **Field Actions:** 1st Top-Dressing: 50% Nitrogen (Urea @ 35 kg/acre) + 5 kg/acre Zinc Sulphate.
- **Water Management:** Maintain 3-5 cm standing water. Practice Alternate Wetting and Drying (AWD) to save 25% water.
### Stage 4: Booting, Heading & Flowering (Day 71 - 95)
- **Field Actions:** 2nd Top-Dressing: Remaining 25% Nitrogen + 50% Potash (MOP @ 15 kg/acre) at panicle initiation.
- **Protection:** Monitor for Brown Planthopper (BPH) at base and Neck Blast on emerging panicles.
### Stage 5: Milky, Dough Stage & Physiological Maturity (Day 96 - 120)
- **Field Actions:** Grain filling occurs. Stop irrigation completely 10-12 days before harvest to allow uniform ripening.
### Stage 6: Harvesting & Threshing (Day 121 - 135)
- **Maturity Index:** 85% of panicles turn golden yellow, and grain moisture drops to 20-22%. Combine harvest and dry grain to 14% moisture for safe storage.

## 4. Scientific Fertilizer Schedule (RDF: 120:60:40 N:P2O5:K2O kg/ha)
- **Basal (Transplanting):** 30 kg N (65 kg Urea) + 60 kg P2O5 (130 kg DAP or 375 kg SSP) + 20 kg K2O (33 kg MOP) + 25 kg ZnSO4/ha.
- **1st Split (Tillering - 25 DAT):** 60 kg N (130 kg Urea/ha).
- **2nd Split (Panicle Initiation - 45 DAT):** 30 kg N (65 kg Urea) + 20 kg K2O (33 kg MOP/ha).

## 5. Integrated Pest & Disease Management (IPM)
- **Yellow Stem Borer (*Scirpophaga incertulas*):**
  - *Symptoms:* "Dead hearts" in vegetative stage; "White ears" in flowering.
  - *ETL:* 2 egg masses/m² or 10% dead hearts.
  - *Control:* Chlorantraniliprole 18.5% SC @ 60 ml/acre or Cartap Hydrochloride 4G @ 10 kg/acre.
- **Brown Planthopper (*Nilaparvata lugens*):**
  - *Symptoms:* "Hopper burn" circular drying patches in dense canopy.
  - *Control:* Pymetrozine 50% WDG @ 120 g/acre or Triflumezopyrim 10% SC @ 94 ml/acre. Direct spray at stem base.
- **Blast (*Pyricularia oryzae*):**
  - *Symptoms:* Spindle-shaped lesions with brown margins and grey center.
  - *Control:* Tricyclazole 75% WP @ 120 g/acre or Isoprothiolane 40% EC @ 300 ml/acre.
