# Crop: Rice (Oryza sativa)

## 1. Agro-Climatic Requirements & Soil Suitability
- **Optimal Soil Types:** Clayey Loam, Alluvial Soil, Heavy Black Soil with high water retention.
- **pH Range:** 5.5 - 7.5.
- **Temperature Thresholds:** Optimal 22°C - 32°C. High humidity (> 70%) during vegetative growth.
- **Water Requirement:** 1100 - 1400 mm total seasonal water.

## 2. Growth Stages & Lifecycle Calendar (Sowing to Harvest: 120-135 Days)
### Stage 1: Nursery Preparation & Seedling Growth (Day 1 - 25)
- **Key Actions:** Treat seed with Carbendazim (2g/kg seed). Apply DAP @ 10 kg/acre in nursery bed. Maintain thin 2 cm water layer.
### Stage 2: Mainfield Transplanting & Early Tillering (Day 26 - 45)
- **Key Actions:** Transplant 2-3 seedlings/hill at 20x15 cm spacing. Apply full P (SSP/DAP) + 50% Potash (MOP) + 25% Nitrogen as Basal. Apply Pretilachlor 50% EC @ 500 ml/acre within 3 days.
### Stage 3: Active Tillering & Panicle Primordia (Day 46 - 75)
- **Key Actions:** 1st Top Dressing: 50% Nitrogen (Urea @ 35 kg/acre) + Zinc Sulphate @ 10 kg/acre. Maintain 2-5 cm standing water.
### Stage 4: Flowering & Grain Filling (Day 76 - 105)
- **Key Actions:** 2nd Top Dressing: Remaining 25% Nitrogen + remaining 50% Potash (MOP @ 15 kg/acre). Monitor for BPH and Neck Blast.
### Stage 5: Ripening & Harvest (Day 106 - 135)
- **Key Actions:** Drain water completely 10 days before harvest. Harvest when 80-85% grains turn golden yellow. Sun-dry to 14% moisture.

## 3. Recommended Fertilizers & Quantities (kg/ha)
- Nitrogen (N): 120 kg/ha (Urea: 260 kg/ha).
- Phosphorus (P2O5): 60 kg/ha (DAP: 130 kg/ha or SSP: 375 kg/ha).
- Potassium (K2O): 40-60 kg/ha (MOP: 70-100 kg/ha).
- Zinc Sulphate: 25 kg/ha.
