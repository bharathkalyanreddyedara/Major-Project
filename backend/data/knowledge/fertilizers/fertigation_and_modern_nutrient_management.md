# Modern Fertigation & Precision Nutrient Management Guide
*Authoritative Reference: AgricultureGuruji & ICAR Directives*

## 1. Principles of Drip Fertigation
Fertigation is the precise application of fully water-soluble fertilizers directly to the crop root zone through a drip irrigation network.
- **Water & Fertilizer Efficiency:** Increases nutrient use efficiency (NUE) by 30-40% compared to broad-casting, and saves 40-50% water.
- **Root Zone Optimization:** Prevents fertilizer leaching into groundwater and maintains consistent soil solution electrical conductivity (EC < 1.5 dS/m).

## 2. Standard 100% Water-Soluble Fertilizers (WSF)
- **19:19:19 (Polyfeed / Balanced):** Used during active vegetative growth and early branching.
- **12:61:0 (Mono Ammonium Phosphate - MAP):** High phosphorus formulation applied during root establishment and early flower bud initiation.
- **0:52:34 (Mono Potassium Phosphate - MKP):** Phosphorus and potassium booster applied from flowering to early fruit set (promotes heavy bloom and prevents flower drop).
- **13:0:45 (Potassium Nitrate):** Applied during fruit enlargement, grain filling, and ripening for maximum size, color development, and Brix sugar accumulation.
- **Calcium Nitrate [15.5% N + 18.8% Ca]:** Mandatory for Solanaceous vegetables (Tomato, Capsicum, Potato) and Pomegranate to prevent Blossom End Rot and fruit cracking.
- **Magnesium Sulphate [9.6% Mg + 12% S]:** Boosts chlorophyll synthesis in cotton, sugarcane, and vegetables.

## 3. General Fertigation Frequency & Guidelines
- **Frequency:** Apply fertilizers in 2 to 3 weekly split doses rather than bulk monthly applications.
- **Compatibility Rules:**
  - *Tank A:* Calcium Nitrate + Iron Chelates (Fe-EDTA).
  - *Tank B:* Phosphates (MKP, MAP) + Sulphates (MgSO4, ZnSO4, SOP).
  - *Rule:* Never mix Calcium Nitrate with Phosphates or Sulphates in the same concentrated stock tank (causes insoluble Calcium Phosphate/Sulphate precipitation clogging emitters).
- **Flushing:** Run plain water for 10-15 minutes after fertigation to flush lines and prevent salt encrustation inside dripper labyrinths.

## 4. Protected Cultivation & Greenhouse Best Practices
- **Temperature & Humidity Management:** Optimal daytime temperature: 24°C - 28°C; relative humidity: 65% - 75%. Use foggers, shade nets (50% shade), and side-curtain ventilation during summer peak.
- **Mulching:** Use 25-micron silver-black reflective polyethylene mulch to suppress 95% weed growth, conserve 30% soil moisture, and repel aphid/whitefly vectors through UV reflection.
