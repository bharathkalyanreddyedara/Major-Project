# Biofertilizers & Microbial Inoculants in Precision Farming

## 1. Nitrogen-Fixing Biofertilizers
- **Rhizobium:** Symbiotic N-fixation in root nodules of pulses and legumes (Chickpea, Soybean, Groundnut, Blackgram, Pigeonpea). Fixes 50 - 100 kg N/ha. Seed treatment @ 200g / 10 kg seeds using jaggery gum.
- **Azotobacter:** Free-living aerobic N-fixer for non-leguminous cereals (Wheat, Maize, Cotton, Mustard, Vegetables). Fixes 20 - 30 kg N/ha and secretes growth-promoting hormones.
- **Azospirillum:** Associative N-fixer in rhizosphere of Rice, Sugarcane, Sorghum, and Millets. Enhances root surface area and drought tolerance.
- **Blue-Green Algae (BGA / Anabaena azollae) & Azolla:** Floating fern applied @ 1 tonne/ha in flooded paddy fields; adds 30-40 kg N/ha and suppresses weeds.

## 2. Phosphorus & Potassium Solubilizers
- **PSB (Phosphate Solubilizing Bacteria - Bacillus megaterium, Pseudomonas striata):** Secretes organic acids (citric, gluconic, succinic) that solubilize fixed insoluble tri-calcium phosphate into bio-available monocalcium phosphate. Application rate: 2.5 kg/ha mixed with 100 kg FYM.
- **VAM (Vesicular-Arbuscular Mycorrhiza - Glomus intraradices):** Symbiotic root fungus whose hyphal network extends root absorption volume by 400%, dramatically increasing uptake of immobile Phosphorus, Zinc, and water.
- **KSB (Potassium Solubilizing Bacteria - Frateuria aurantia):** Solubilizes structural potassium from mica and feldspar minerals.

## 3. Best Practices for Biofertilizer Inoculation
- Never mix biofertilizer inoculants directly with chemical fertilizers or synthetic fungicides.
- Maintain at least 48 hours gap between chemical seed treatment (e.g., Thiram, Imidacloprid) and biofertilizer seed coating.
