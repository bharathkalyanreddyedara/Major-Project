# Primary Macro-Nutrient (NPK) Management & Quantitative Deficit Calculations

## 1. Commercial Fertilizer Specifications
- **Urea:** 46% Nitrogen (N). Grade: 46-0-0. Formula: `Urea (kg) = Deficit Nitrogen (kg) * (100 / 46) = Deficit N * 2.17`.
- **DAP (Di-Ammonium Phosphate):** 18% Nitrogen (N) + 46% Phosphate (P2O5). Grade: 18-46-0. Formula: `DAP (kg) = Deficit P2O5 (kg) * (100 / 46) = Deficit P * 2.17`. (Note: DAP also supplies `DAP_kg * 0.18` kg of Nitrogen).
- **MOP (Muriate of Potash / Potassium Chloride):** 60% Potash (K2O). Grade: 0-0-60. Formula: `MOP (kg) = Deficit K2O (kg) * (100 / 60) = Deficit K * 1.67`.
- **SSP (Single Super Phosphate):** 16% Phosphate (P2O5) + 11% Sulphur (S) + 19% Calcium (Ca). Grade: 0-16-0. Ideal for oilseeds and pulse crops requiring Sulphur. Formula: `SSP (kg) = Deficit P2O5 (kg) * 6.25`.
- **NPK Complex 10:26:26:** Balanced basal complex for tubers, vegetables, and commercial crops.
- **NPK Complex 20:20:0:13:** Excellent for oilseed crops requiring equal N and P plus 13% Sulphur.

## 2. Quantitative Deficit Calculation Method (kg/ha)
1. **Determine Crop Target Requirement:** (Standard recommended dose of fertilizer - RDF for target yield).
2. **Measure Soil Test Level:** Lab report available N, P, K in kg/ha.
3. **Calculate Soil Available Fraction:** High soil test (> threshold) -> reduce RDF by 25%; Low soil test (< threshold) -> increase RDF by 25%.
4. **Deficit = Target Requirement - Available Soil Fraction.**
5. **Convert to Commercial Bags (50 kg bags).**

## 3. Application Timings & 4R Nutrient Stewardship
- **Right Source:** Match fertilizer type to soil pH (e.g., avoid DAP on alkaline soils where surface ammonia volatilization is high; use SSP or band-placed DAP).
- **Right Rate:** Base applications on soil testing rather than routine blank applications.
- **Right Time:** Split Nitrogen application into Basal (25-33%), Active Tillering / Branching (33-50%), and Flowering / Panicle Initiation (25-33%). Never apply entire N as basal.
- **Right Place:** Band place P and K 5 cm below seed depth where root intercept is highest.
