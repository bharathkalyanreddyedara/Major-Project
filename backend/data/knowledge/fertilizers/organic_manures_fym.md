# Organic Manures, Composts & Soil Organic Carbon Enrichment

## 1. Primary Organic Manure Types & Nutrient Profiles
- **Well-Rotted Farm Yard Manure (FYM):** Contains ~0.5% N, 0.25% P2O5, 0.5% K2O. Application rate: 10-15 tonnes/ha (Field crops), 20-25 tonnes/ha (Vegetables & Orchards).
- **Vermicompost:** Earthworm-processed organic compost rich in humic acids, growth hormones (auxins, cytokinins), and beneficial microbes. Contains 1.5 - 2.5% N, 1.0 - 1.8% P2O5, 1.5 - 2.0% K2O. Application rate: 2.5 - 5.0 tonnes/ha.
- **Poultry Manure:** Highly concentrated in Nitrogen and Phosphorus (3.0% N, 2.5% P2O5, 1.5% K2O). Must be well-decomposed before application to prevent ammonia root burn. Application rate: 2 - 3 tonnes/ha.
- **Neem Cake / Castor Cake:** Organic non-edible oil cakes that act as natural nitrification inhibitors (slows down urea conversion to nitrate, preventing leaching) and nematicides. Application rate: 250 - 500 kg/ha.

## 2. Green Manuring Protocols
- **In-situ Green Manuring:** Grow Dhaincha (Sesbania aculeata) or Sunnhemp (Crotalaria juncea) @ 50 kg seed/ha; incorporate into the soil with a disc plough at 45 days (50% flowering stage) before puddling. Adds 60-80 kg organic Nitrogen per hectare.
- **Green Leaf Manuring:** Incorporate green leaves and loppings of Gliricidia sepium, Pongamia pinnata, or Subabul into field trenches @ 5 tonnes/ha.

## 3. Soil Organic Carbon (SOC) Rebuilding Strategy
- **Critical Threshold:** Soil Organic Carbon should be maintained > 0.75% for optimal biological fertility and moisture retention.
- **Combined INM Approach:** 75% chemical RDF + 25% organic manure reduces long-term synthetic fertilizer degradation and enhances beneficial mycorrhizae.
