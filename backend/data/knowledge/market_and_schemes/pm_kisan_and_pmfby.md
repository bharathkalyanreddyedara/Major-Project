# Agricultural Credit, Insurance (PMFBY) & Government Welfare Schemes

## 1. Pradhan Mantri Fasal Bima Yojana (PMFBY)
- **Premium Rates for Farmers:** Kharif crops: 2.0% of sum insured; Rabi crops: 1.5% of sum insured; Annual Commercial & Horticultural crops: 5.0% of sum insured.
- **Coverage:** Prevented sowing, mid-season localized adversities (hailstorm, landslide, inundation), post-harvest losses (up to 14 days after harvest for crops in cut-and-spread condition).
- **Claim Timeline:** Report localized crop loss within 72 hours via the Crop Insurance App or local agricultural officer.

## 2. Soil Health Card (SHC) & PM-KISAN
- **Soil Health Card:** Provides 12 nutrient status indicators (N, P, K, S, Zn, Fe, Cu, Mn, B, pH, EC, OC) with tailored crop-specific fertilizer recommendations.
- **Kisan Credit Card (KCC):** Concessional institutional credit at 4% effective interest rate (with 3% prompt repayment subvention) up to ₹3,00,000 for inputs and working capital.
