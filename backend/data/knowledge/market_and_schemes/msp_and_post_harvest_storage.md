# Minimum Support Price (MSP), Mandi Procurement & Post-Harvest Storage

## 1. Grain Moisture Standards for Government Procurement
- **Paddy (Rice):** Maximum permissible moisture content: 17.0%.
- **Wheat:** Maximum permissible moisture content: 12.0%.
- **Maize:** Maximum permissible moisture content: 14.0%.
- **Cotton:** Moisture content should not exceed 8.0% - 12.0% with staple length verified.
- **Soybean / Pulses:** Maximum moisture content: 12.0%.

## 2. Post-Harvest Cleaning & Hermetic Storage
- **Sun Drying:** Dry harvested grain on clean tarpaulins in thin layers (5 cm), turning every 2 hours until target moisture is reached.
- **Hermetic Storage (PICS Bags):** Store grains in Purdue Improved Crop Storage (PICS) multi-layer hermetic bags to prevent Bruchids, Grain Weevils (Sitophilus oryzae), and mold without chemical insecticides.
- **Warehouse Receipt System (e-NWR):** Deposit certified produce in WDRA-registered warehouses to access pledge loans up to 75% of produce value during market price dips.
