# Weather Advisory: Heavy Rainfall, Storm & Waterlogging Management

## 1. Meteorological Trigger Conditions
- Forecasted 24-hour precipitation > 40 mm OR wind speed > 35 km/h OR cloudburst warnings.

## 2. Pre-Rain Action Plan
- **Drainage Prep:** Clear farm ditches, unblock drainage outlets, and create escape furrows around field boundaries.
- **Suspend Sprays:** Immediately postpone scheduled pesticide, herbicide, and foliar fertilizer sprays (spray wash-off occurs if rain falls within 6-8 hours).
- **Propping:** Provide bamboo support/earthing-up to tall lodging-prone crops (Sugarcane, Banana, Maize).

## 3. Post-Flood Recovery Protocol
- **Drainage:** Evacuate standing floodwater within 24 to 48 hours to prevent root rot and anaerobic soil hypoxia.
- **Foliar Nutrition:** Root uptake capacity is severely impaired following waterlogging. Apply foliar spray of 1% Urea + 1% Multi-NPK (19:19:19) or 1% Potassium Nitrate (KNO3) to stimulate quick recovery.
- **Fungal Prevention:** Drench root zones with Copper Oxychloride 50% WP @ 3g/L or Metalaxyl @ 2g/L to prevent Phytophthora/Pythium collar rot.
