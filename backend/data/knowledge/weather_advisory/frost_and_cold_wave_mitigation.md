# Weather Advisory: Frost & Cold Wave Protection Protocols

## 1. Meteorological Trigger Conditions
- Minimum ambient temperature dropping below 4°C with clear skies and calm winds (Radiational Frost).

## 2. Emergency Mitigation Actions
- **Night Irrigation:** Provide light surface irrigation during the evening; water has a high specific heat capacity, keeping soil temperatures 2-3°C warmer through the night.
- **Smudging / Smoke Screens:** Burn dry agricultural waste, weeds, and cow dung cakes along the windward perimeter of orchards between 2:00 AM and 6:00 AM to create a protective thermal blanket.
- **Chemical Spray:** Spray 0.1% Thiourea or 0.1% Sulphuric Acid (1 ml H2SO4 per litre of water) to enhance cold-shock protein tolerance.
