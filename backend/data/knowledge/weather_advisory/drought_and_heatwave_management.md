# Weather Advisory: Drought, Dry Spell & Heatwave Management

## 1. Meteorological Trigger Conditions
- Temperature > 38°C (Plains) or > 30°C (Hills) OR rainfall deficit > 50% for 14 consecutive days during critical growth stages.

## 2. Crop Protection Strategies
- **Anti-Transpirant Spray:** Spray Potassium Nitrate (KNO3 @ 1%) or Kaolin clay (5%) or Sodium Metabisulphite to reflect solar radiation and induce stomatal closure.
- **Alternate Furrow Irrigation:** In row crops (Cotton, Maize, Sugarcane), irrigate alternate furrows to stretch limited water across double the acreage.
- **Mulching:** Spread crop residue, dry grass, or shredded straw (5 tonnes/ha) between rows to retain soil moisture and reduce soil surface temperatures by 4-6°C.
- **Micro-Irrigation Scheduling:** Shift drip irrigation timings to late evening or night to eliminate daytime evaporation losses.
